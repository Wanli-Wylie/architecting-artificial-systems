# Architecting artificial systems

An artificial system is constructed, organized or maintained in relation to purposes that its mechanisms alone do not give. Kant's notion of *Zweck* (purpose) is an orientation here rather than a part of the model: what a system is for cannot be read off how it works. A balancing authority's control of its part of a power grid, a hospital's consult workflow, an operating system, an army, a national air traffic control service and a national data exchange are artificial systems in this sense, and so is the team that designs any of them.

This Skill is about architecting a system under one shared governance: a common order, carried by law, charters, directives, adopted standards and contracts and by the bodies that maintain them, under which roles carry authority and responsibility and what actors cannot settle among themselves has somewhere to go. Within it, actors hold distributed information, capabilities and decisions, and they often organize in two ways at once. Vertically, an actor turns one of its actions into tasks for other actors, who may delegate in turn, so that a unit can act as one actor for the command above it. Horizontally, actors request and provide services, draw on shared resources under allocation, share information and support one another. The two structures need not match, since a unit carrying out a delegated task typically also deals with actors elsewhere, and none of this implies central control or a global planner. Many systems show only one of the two, or neither, and a good reading says so.

Designing the order among several independent authorities is a different subject, which this Skill does not treat; the independent organizations a system serves, buys from or depends on appear in its design as clients, suppliers or environment. This states the Skill's scope, not a test a system has to pass, and where governance is partial or contested the Skill is used as far as it helps.

The Skill looks at a system at two layers. At the metaphysical layer a system is what it is in its own terms: the things it deals in and how they relate, what happens to them, who acts and who answers for what, how work is handed down and how it is coordinated across. At the physical layer it is whatever carries that out: programs, machines, networks and stores, devices and physical processes, and people and organizations as they behave. Each part of the physical layer works by its own field's principles, those of computer science, of management and organizations, or of physics. The Skill contains none of them and is agnostic about which realize a given system, but it never forgets that something does: whatever the metaphysical layer describes happens only through something physical, whose principles limit what the design can be. The metaphysical layer directs and the physical layer bounds. The system's hierarchies, of abstraction, assembly, distributed execution, delegation and control over physical dynamics, all sit within the first, and somewhere each design stops describing and takes a part as given by its contract.

What sits at the metaphysical layer is made. A consult's being Pending or a force's being under one commander's operational control exists because people and programs treat it as existing and act on it, under an order that says what counts as what, and what is made could be made otherwise, which is what lets it be designed. So the Skill speaks of what a system holds as records, readings, statuses, estimates, claims and commitments, each with an origin, and of anything that matters it asks how it comes to be and who may change it. It does not appeal to "fact" or "truth", and a design written with it does well not to either: those words point past every record and reading to a ground nothing in the system provides, and they close the question of how a thing came to be just where it should open.

Architecture works to make the two layers fit. Important requirements have actors able to meet them, decisions have the information and authority they need, acts have a way to happen and to be seen to have happened, and constraints across actors have a place where they take effect. Architecture also gives each structure a reason, such as a requirement met, a boundary spanned, a decision informed, a failure contained, a load spread or a change made independent. Structure costs building, consistency and operation, so designs tend to stay capable when what can be derived is derived, no two places change one thing with nothing settling between them, and a premise is revised once a family of mechanisms has grown around it.

Use this Skill to design, review, diagnose or change such a system. It offers a way of thinking about systems, the activities around a system, and sixteen cases of real systems read in that way. It is written as inquiry, not as a formal system. Its words, such as actor, role, task, delegation, request and allocation, are offered where they help, a field's own words are kept where they serve better, and nothing in it is a checklist. A design can read well at every step and still be the wrong design.

## The model in brief

[references/model.md](references/model.md) works the model out through the cases, in ten parts.

Requirements reach a design in stakeholders' words, with a scope and a bound, and kept apart from the means that meet them, they let those means change. A measure binds where someone relies on it, and otherwise indicates something it captures in part and is revised when it drifts, as NERC replaced CPS2 once it no longer pointed at frequency ([grid](references/cases/grid-balancing.md)). A requirement can come from beyond the governance boundary, and can be served without being enforced ([X-Road](references/cases/x-road-estonia.md)).

At the metaphysical layer, what a thing is and who may act on it do not come apart: a consult is partly who may receive, forward and complete it, and a mission partly the intent that bounds what a subordinate may choose. Entities and relations, acts and actors, responsibilities and capabilities are therefore one inquiry, carried on in each field's own words where it has them. Some things a system makes by its own acts, such as a status, a quota grant or a command relationship. Others it can know only by reading, such as the care a consult is meant to bring about or an airplane's angle of attack, and much trouble comes from taking a status for the care it records ([VistA consults](references/cases/vista-consults.md)) or one vane's reading for the angle itself ([MCAS](references/cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md)).

The physical layer bounds what the metaphysical layer directs. SAP's classic ledger kept totals beside its line items because summing on disk was slow, and that constraint built totals tables, update steps and reconciliation into the model itself; in-memory columns removed it and the family went with it ([SAP](references/cases/sap-universal-journal.md)). A balancing authority builds on inertia and governors it did not design and nobody decides in the moment ([grid](references/cases/grid-balancing.md)). Toyota's Georgetown plant and a Ford plant at Dearborn had similar rope and lamp, and workers pulled the cord about 2,000 times a week at one and about twice at the other, because of how pulls had been received ([andon](references/cases/toyota-andon.md)). People appear at both layers: doctrine grants a commander authority, and whether subordinates then choose as the commander would rests on trust and training built over time ([mission command](references/cases/mission-command.md)).

Of anything a system keeps that matters, the useful question is how it comes to be and who may change it. Naming one place authoritative answers only whom to read from: GCCS-J's instruction makes the whole system the "system of record" and says nothing of what alters a track or settles two theatre pictures that disagree ([GCCS-J](references/cases/gccs-j.md)). Many designs give each thing one place where it changes and have everyone else ask, as X-Road leaves an address with the population register, Borg lets only its master change the state of a cell ([Borg](references/cases/borg.md)) and joint doctrine lets no two commanders hold the same relationship over one force at once ([joint command](references/cases/joint-command-support.md)). Where several must be able to change one thing, the better designs make the settling an act of its own, as Kubernetes' server-side apply refuses one manager's change to a field another manages unless it is forced ([Kubernetes](references/cases/kubernetes-controllers.md)). What creates work is two places that can each change one thing with nothing settling between them, as when one SAP vendor invoice took "fifteen consecutive steps".

Under one governance, the vertical and horizontal structures each get their own reasons. Joint doctrine runs operational command and administrative control side by side, the FAA's Command Center directs traffic management through units inside facilities that answer to their own line ([FAA](references/cases/faa-air-traffic.md)), and Borg's priority bands rank kinds of work, not teams. Decisions generally go where information and the ability to change the outcome meet, centralized where consistency, fairness or scale outweigh local knowledge, and authority given to whoever holds the information works when a response stands behind it, as an andon pull needs a team leader who comes.

Feedback loops run at tempos that overlap, and physics and fixed control laws often carry the first seconds before anyone can choose. A reading is not the thing read, and it is worth what the process behind it is worth. Intent, acceptance, progress and outcome are distinct, as a VHA consult moves from Pending to Active to Complete, and an unknown outcome is usually kept unknown and observed again, as Kubernetes marks a quiet node Unknown and waits before evicting.

Layers have many reasons, each repaying the interface, failure mode and latency a layer costs, and the end-to-end argument places a function where it can be completed and checked ([end-to-end](references/cases/end-to-end.md)). Delegation carries latitude, authority and a sense of what counts as done for the whole, as mission command's intent "describes what constitutes success". Where layers act on one thing, each needs to know what the other may do, which MCAS shows by its absence: its authority over the stabilizer grew while the manuals, training and safety assessment stayed behind. Across the delegation structure, actors coordinate through requests under contracts, allocation under an enforced rule, shared records, support set up by a common superior and agreements, and the governance gives what they cannot settle a place to go. A join between separately owned parts works when it has a meaning and an owner of its own.

Somewhere a design stops describing and takes a part as given, and the outcomes that part's contract admits are the design's real assumption about it; MCAS took one vane, and the crew's reaction within seconds, as given. Structure that makes a property hold usually costs less than a check, as Unix's rule against links to directories protects programs that walk the tree ([Multics and Unix](references/cases/multics-unix.md)), and checks earn their place where separately owned parts meet. A design is read from its requirements down and from its structure up, and a premise that a family of mechanisms serves is revised as one: on the 737 MAX the premise that crews would retrim within seconds served the hazard classification, the simulator method, the single sensor and the manuals at once. Premises like that are often physical, about what a disk can sum or how fast a person reacts, and they are the ones a design most easily forgets it holds.

## Activities

The activities interleave and are not stages, and a session goes better when it says which activity it is in and, on switching, why. [references/activities.md](references/activities.md) describes what good work in each looks like.

Formulating what is required names stakeholders, requirements, measures and the things and actions that matter, starting from the governance the system is under. What is asked is a representation and can be wrong, a need stated in the shape of a mechanism is separated from the restriction it implies, and the premises most often wrong are about what carries the system.

Architecting decides roles, what is delegated and what is coordinated horizontally, layers and boundaries, how the state that matters comes to be and who may change it, and what the design leans on in whatever carries it out, working from a draft design a reader can argue with. Each structure gets its own reasons rather than being read off another's shape, and saying which structures a design has, and which it lacks, is part of the design.

Realizing builds, procures and reuses, and reads construction as evidence, since a field added to keep a concept alive can mean unfinished code or a design asking for what need not exist. A reused part is asked again about its new environment, and a reused practice about the people who will carry it.

Operating and observing runs the loops at tempos matched to what they act on and keeps each indicator beside what it indicates, so that a measure does not take a goal's place (Merton's goal displacement). A check belongs to what it serves, and kept for any other reason it is ceremony.

Diagnosing and reformulating reads a difficulty three ways before repairing it: a capability the system lacks, a design demanding what need not exist, or a formulation producing the difficulty.

Evolving changes a running system at its premises rather than trimming mechanisms one by one, says what each removal leaves holding and what now carries it, and keeps compatibility as a contract with clients who cannot change in step.

Describing a design writes for people and agents who will act on it. It says plainly which statements ask, gives the reasons so that a reader can act where the text runs out, states each thing once where it is owned, and says where it stops; a description that tries to fix every word in advance is read as a form to comply with.

Organizing the work treats the design effort as an artificial system of its own, with people and AI agents as its actors, briefs that say what success is and leave the means to whoever does the work, one shared working document that guides thinking without hardening into a specification, and only questions of meaning sent up to whoever holds the meaning.

## Cases

Each case reads one real system or design from public sources: how its things come to be and who may change them, how its actors are organized, what carries it out, and where the reading adds little. The Skill teaches through cases because, as Naur argued, the theory behind a design cannot be fully recovered from its artifacts.

- [Palantir Foundry Ontology](references/cases/palantir-ontology.md): part of an organization's model made to run, with feeds and edits as two ways a property changes, and "done" as a configuration choice.
- [SAP Universal Journal](references/cases/sap-universal-journal.md): stored totals as a cost of the machine built into the model, removed as one family when that cost changed, with compatibility views as a thin contract.
- [An X-Road instance](references/cases/x-road-estonia.md): members admitted into a standard's roles, data changed only where it is kept, configuration copies kept by signature and expiry, and an exchange with its own owner.
- [Mission command](references/cases/mission-command.md): delegation by intent, the trust and training that carry it, and doctrine on when tighter control fits.
- [GCCS-J](references/cases/gccs-j.md): a shared picture operated and secured along separate lines, and a "system of record" that says whom to read from and not how a track comes to be.
- [US joint command and support relationships](references/cases/joint-command-support.md): command relationships made by named acts, so that one commander at a time directs a force in a given way, and support set up by a common superior.
- [The FAA's air traffic organization](references/cases/faa-air-traffic.md): two vertical structures, handoffs that keep one controller per aircraft, letters of agreement between facilities, and an order that defines its verbs.
- [VistA consults](references/cases/vista-consults.md): services requesting and accepting work through a record whose statuses separate intent, acceptance, progress and outcome, and a directive that keeps the record tied to the care.
- [A balancing authority](references/cases/grid-balancing.md): physics, controllers and dispatchers at overlapping tempos, one agreed meter at each boundary, and requirements from beyond the governance boundary.
- [Kubernetes controllers](references/cases/kubernetes-controllers.md): delegation as desired state and observed status, and field managers, conflicts and resource versions settling who may change what.
- [Borg](references/cases/borg.md): allocation by priority, quota, admission and placement, a master that alone changes a cell's state, and machines that act when memory runs out.
- [Multics to Unix](references/cases/multics-unix.md): a layer by interface, who held a program deciding where a function went, and a change of what was asked, with its cost.
- [The end-to-end argument](references/cases/end-to-end.md): a function placed where it can be completed and checked, a thin contract, the failures of real networks, and fate-sharing.
- [Toyota andon](references/cases/toyota-andon.md): authority to signal an abnormality, made usable by a team leader who answers, and the same hardware carried differently by different people.
- [Google SRE's service level objectives](references/cases/sre-slos.md): a measure as a binding requirement, and an error budget that two teams act on by agreement.
- [Boeing 737 MAX MCAS](references/cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md): the one failure case, an automated layer whose authority grew while the crew's knowledge stayed behind, with one sensor's reading taken for the angle itself.

## Revising this Skill

The Skill is itself a representation, drawn from sixteen cases, and fitting them does not show that it fits the next system. It is meant to change when applying it produces a worse design or misses a decision that mattered, and the reason can be stated generally; one surprise is a case to keep, a pattern across cases a reason to revise. Revise it as it revises a design: change a premise rather than add a special case, say what a removed passage protected and what protects it now, and watch for its return under another name. Rewrite the affected text whole, with no history inside it, add the case to `references/cases/` where public sources can carry it, and add a row to [CHANGES.md](CHANGES.md).
