# Activities

Work on an artificial system moves among kinds of attention: saying what is required, deciding structure, building, running, finding out why a difficulty returns, changing what already runs, writing the design down, and organizing the people and AI agents who do this. The activities interleave and are not stages. Formulating and building change each other and neither finishes first: a draft design exposes a requirement nobody stated, and a requirement found late reorganizes the draft. A session goes better when it says which activity it is in, at what tempo and what will carry its result to the others, and on switching, what the previous activity was doing and why that is no longer the question. The division is a lens, like the model in [model.md](model.md), and each activity is described by what good work in it tends to look like. Where no case or source is named, the text states judgment from practice.

## Formulating what is required

Formulating names the stakeholders and what they require, the measures that bind and the indicators that inform, and the objects and actions of the logical world that matter, and it decides what is left out. The governance the system is under settles what is being designed, so it is a natural place to start. An X-Road instance is what one governing authority administers, with its members as clients ([X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)), and a balancing authority works among generators and neighbours that are its environment ([grid](cases/grid-balancing.md)). Naming each outside organization with what the system relies on from it frames the work without testing whether the system qualifies, and where governance is partial the frame is used as far as it helps.

Requirements are best gathered in the words of those who rely on them, and they are usually few. SRE advises starting from "what your users care about, not what you can measure" and preferring "a loose target that you tighten" to current performance ([SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)). A property made a requirement because it is easy to check tends to drift toward stricter and costlier demands, and once anything is a requirement, debate turns to meeting it more cheaply. Asking which stakeholder would notice its absence, and whose failure it prevents, cuts both ways, since a measure users rely on binds like any requirement, with a bound that may be a ceiling as well as a minimum.

Stakeholders tend to state needs in the shape of the mechanism they expect, and separating the restriction a need implies from that mechanism leaves room for a better one. The end-to-end paper warns that a subsystem specified before its applications are known tempts its designer "to 'help' the users by taking on more function than necessary" ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)).

Much of a formulation was attached by an earlier solution, a neighbouring domain or a convenient representation. What an independent second implementation would need, and whether the structure would emerge if the system were built today, help sort it. Removing an attached element means naming what it was attached to, and one attachment usually carried several things (see [Evolving](#evolving)). An imported frame brings its hard problems, such as transactions and exactly-once delivery. Its lesson about observation is usually worth keeping and its promises only where a stakeholder needs them, as the datagram leaves loss visible and reliability to the ends that want it.

What is asked is a representation and can be wrong, so it is held loosely enough to change without losing the work done under it. A formulation tends to be mature when its parts can be named in existing fields and asked of the world, since parts checkable only against the formulation breed concepts that explain one another. Consistency among its answers is a property of the representation, and maps of the problem space are lenses and coverage instruments, not parts lists, which no test run inside them can test. Requirements that hold only under conditions no stakeholder has committed to have not yet touched the world, and an assumptions section reading "none" usually means nobody looked. Writing down what no section checks shows where a formulation is newest, and study of mature systems gives it footing.

A decided absence is stated once, with its cost, where the requirement it limits lives. An unreached one is left unstated, so that it hardens neither into a prohibition nor into a speculative mechanism. The order of building is not part of what the system is.

## Architecting

Architecting decides roles and actors, what is delegated and what is coordinated horizontally, layers, boundaries, sources of truth, and the mechanisms through which actors share facts, coordinate and observe. It goes best from a draft design, a concrete arrangement a reader can argue with (actors with tasks, messages with fields, states with transitions), held as the reference questions are asked of rather than as a verdict. Inherited problems are restated plainly and asked of the draft, and one that lives nowhere in it came from the old form. Each gets its own finding. Findings often say that the world dissolves the problem, an existing element carries it, it expands the design, it does not arise (and under what condition it would), another owner holds it, or it lies outside; these are what findings usually look like, and a kind with no finding is not a gap. Inherited concepts are not mapped onto components one to one.

Under one governance, delegation and the horizontal relations of services, requests, allocation and support each get their own reasons, neither read off the other's shape and neither needing a planner over the whole; the model's [three perspectives](model.md#three-perspectives-on-one-system) give the cases. Saying which structures a design has, and which it lacks, is part of the design. Roles defined elsewhere, in a standard, a regulation or an existing interface, are adopted where they fit rather than redefined, the design says how its governance gives each one authority, and an adopted role holds while the actors in it change.

Delegation is designed with what it carries, latitude written into the task, authority in stated amounts and a rule for reading what returns ([layers and delegation](model.md#layers-and-delegation)), and where each decision goes is asked of the draft, along with whether asking who decides removes an agreement protocol ([actors](model.md#actors)). A shared resource usually needs an allocator and a rule, and a constraint across actors a place where it takes effect, both of which trimming a design can remove unnoticed. Sources of truth, derivation and runtime identity are settled as the [logical world](model.md#the-logical-world) describes, and boundaries tend to follow changes of ownership, execution, trust or lifetime.

Each layer and component is asked what it is for ([correspondence and economy](model.md#correspondence-and-economy)). Asking who would be worse off without an element, and what a user would notice if it were gone, finds the parts whose reason nobody can name, a good place to look when a design is heavy, though the question serves understanding and is no verdict. A mechanism meeting demand it was not built for can have its foundation widened, move to another layer, owner or moment, be absorbed by the world or an existing element, or retire with its carrier named, as Unix's uniform file interface, already used by every command, made the pipe possible. Who relies on the mechanism, whether the world carries it, who would notice its absence, where it has to live and whether it is one of a family are useful questions, roughly in that order, and any answer can end the inquiry.

## Realizing

Realizing builds, procures and reuses, and construction is evidence about the formulation. Fields added to keep a concept alive, mirror records, mappings that preserve an identity and a second lifecycle beside a native one can mean unfinished code, and as often a design asking for what need not exist. The first reading adds objects and comes by default; the second removes work and has to be asked for. Either conclusion lands in the design. Evidence also returns through a question whose premise was examined, a flow traced until a step cannot be written, a survey of real use, and the places where effort gathers.

Real paths are traced in time order through every part they touch, failure and restart included, and a stop is modelled rather than explained away. The 737 MAX's simulator evaluation induced MCAS's stabilizer motion "regardless of underlying cause", so a wrong angle-of-attack reading arriving with stick shaker and alerts never reached a pilot in testing, and neither did the widened 2.5 degree authority ([MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md)). A reused part is asked again about the environment it now serves.

What runs first tends to become the architecture. Unix kept features "because they represented small, easily-coded changes to what existed", and growth by what is cheap can leave an accident that later gets a reason ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)). The judgment drawn here is that large coherent steps toward the final form usually serve better than placeholders or minimal versions, while pilots and staged rollouts answer questions about the world. A stub lets a missing owner look present, so an unbuilt capability is better left visibly unbuilt.

Construction takes definitions, a few requirements and a draft design rather than a list to realize item by item, and its unit of work is a change to the draft in the draft's terms. The frontier is the unbuilt parts whose prerequisites already work, and code that contradicts the design belongs to it. Mature comparators are evidence with a stated confidence, the behaviour of unchecked systems is not invented, and among comparable technologies the selection can rest on operating burden, familiarity and licence. Progress is what the product can now do, in final form and connected to the rest; counts of the formulation's own nouns, stubs, harnesses and renames are activity, and a cycle without progress calls for a change of approach.

## Operating and observing

Operating runs the feedback loops and keeps requirements met over time, each loop at a tempo matched to what it acts on ([feedback loops](model.md#feedback-loops-and-observation)), and many troubles in operation are decisions taken at the wrong tempo. Operation also keeps behaviour near the contract, since "users build on the reality of what you offer, rather than what you say you'll supply" ([SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)).

Indicators are read with what they indicate and revisited when they drift, and a status that measures read needs defending against changes that move the number without the work ([what is required](model.md#artificial-systems-and-what-is-required-of-them)). A measure, check or record that comes to define what is correct beside the design and the product can take the place of what the task is for, Merton's goal displacement, and pass while the capability is absent. The concern is a metric supplanting what the task is for, not measures as constraints on a design.

A verification draws its standing from what it is for: one that confirms an implementation serves the implementation, one delivered to users is product, and one at a boundary is design. A filled schema or a settled status may answer a neighbouring question, as an andon board records a call, a response and a stop, not a defect fixed ([andon](cases/toyota-andon.md)).

Patterns in the work are observations too: effort gathering on one mechanism, a difficulty returning after repair, one cost dominating discussion. They show only to someone who holds the whole. Filling every gap has no stopping point of its own, and a sensible one is where going further prevents nobody's failure.

## Diagnosing and reformulating

A difficulty can be read three ways before it is repaired: as a capability the system lacks, as a design demanding what need not exist, or as a formulation producing the difficulty, in one question or in the whole problem. These are places to intervene, chosen by cost and by damage to what depends on them, with a running system's transition cost counted separately. The default reading is "unfinished", so the other two are asked for on purpose. If a much stronger capability would not change the judgment, the difficulty is not one of capability. Since difficulty is relative to a solver, changing the statement is a legitimate lever.

A question carries the premise of the structure that raised it, and the dissolving option is often among the choices with no reason given to take it. A procedure that picks among the options on the table cannot reach an answer above them: when every option couples two lifetimes, the question is whether the coupling should exist. Effort concentrated on one mechanism, or a requirement defended by measuring it better, points at the requirement rather than at a better mechanism. Other signs that the formulation is at fault include one tension under several vocabularies, one word naming two problems, a requirement no stakeholder relies on, an agreement problem where only one actor decides, a limit gathered at a join, and a status answering an adjacent question. A sign says where to look and proves nothing, and it is repaired where it points rather than where it was noticed; a channel that keeps receiving the wrong kind of question needs a criterion on the channel. Where the whole formulation is suspect, asking who relies on the property, in their words, and what they would lose finds firm ground.

Pressure can be relieved by adding a capability, removing a representation or relinquishing a requirement, each hard to see from the one before, and tests that remove objects never remove a bound. Revising a premise that a family of mechanisms shares is worth more than trimming each member, as the 737 MAX's premise about crew retrim shows ([correspondence and economy](model.md#correspondence-and-economy)).

Reformulation changes what is asked or how it is represented, and its forms are tested differently. A re-rendering asks the same question more cheaply and is tested by equivalence. A replacement answers a different question in the original's service and is tested by whether the answer transfers back: the end-to-end argument replaces the question of how reliable the network should be with the question of who can establish that the function was done, whose answer holds whatever the network does and leaves reliability a question of performance ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)). A change of what is asked is tested by whether the stakeholders who rely on the answer accept it. Unix asked what a small group needed to program together on a cheap machine rather than what a general utility required, and its test was groups at Bell Laboratories taking it up, the Patent department "the first of many groups at the Laboratories to ratify our work" ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)). One change can take different forms for different stakeholders: read this way, SAP's derived balances are a re-rendering for those who read balances and a change for programs written against the old tables ([SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)). Misnaming the form claims an equivalence that does not hold or skips a check the change could have passed.

A reformulation can lose part of the original, so it is checked against what the original demanded, naming what the removed thing silently supplied: Unix gave up named shared segments, dynamic linking, user-visible locks and accounting. Too broad a reformulation cannot be acted on, and too narrow treats symptoms. The moves are open, and common ones fold a freedom into a definition, separate fused roles, dissolve a construct into a relation, move a choice to the layer that meets it, replace a resident controller with a decision re-made over durable state, widen, relax, restate the requirements, move a question to its owner, remove a decider, keep the observation and drop the delivery promise, or demote a specification to a map. A question moved to an owner moves nothing unless the owner exists and can use it, a property moved into a producer's precondition becomes an unchecked exposure, and a representation that replaces a removed one needs the same scrutiny, so a better ontology is not by itself the answer.

Reformulation is not error correction. The earlier formulation was what the work needed, its products are kept as material, and a large model can be demoted to a map without being rewritten. A short record where the decision is made usually says what premise changed, what falls away, what survives and where, what weakened and what the original demanded, and the form the change takes for each stakeholder; earlier records stay as written. A change without a changed premise tends to be a patch, and one without a record a rename.

Escalation follows consequence for meaning, not difficulty or disagreement. Engineering choices, consequences of the design and facts to research are settled by whoever does the work, and a choice of meaning goes to its holder with the options, their costs, what it blocks and a default under which work continues; a lone designer still marks such choices. Before escalating or answering, it is worth asking whether what the question assumes should exist.

## Evolving

A running system changes best at its premises. Mechanisms come in families that share one, and revising the premise removes the family and explains each member, while trimming members one by one leaves the premise to grow them back. SAP's totals, indexes, update steps and reconciliation all rested on the cost of summing on disk, and when in-memory columns removed that cost the premise was revised once ([SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)). The affected part is then rewritten, not deleted clause by clause.

A removal states what still has to hold, what carries it now, what silently relied on the removed thing and what a user loses; "nothing", for something relied on, is the warning, and a removal that would reopen an irreversible effect on a real resource is worth refusing. Removed things regrow under new names, as smaller versions or by moving, and a rename is not a revision; a changed premise prevents regrowth, and a list of withdrawn things with their likely disguises catches it. Over-defence adds mechanisms, over-trimming silently leaves a real resource outside every rule, and losses gather at the joins with real resources. Removals are judged against what has to hold between stakeholders, over time and after loss, and someone who did not make a revision reads it and tries to put things back.

Invariants are written with the failure each prevents, as Unix gives its rule that each directory appears in exactly one other "to simplify the writing of programs which visit subtrees" ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)), and restated in each revision rather than referred to. A reformulation that touches an invariant makes a new system and is better said to. As judgment, invariants are settled first under a narrow space of action, which is then widened without reopening them.

Widening a foundation tends to serve better than adding special cases, and a widening is measured by what the system can then express. Special mechanisms wait for need, while foundations that keep change cheap do not: the datagram's thin contract, "the minimum network service assumption", let one protocol carry a reliable stream and real-time speech over networks from satellite to local area nets ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)). An absence from today's build is not a prohibition, and policy kept apart from its mechanism changes without touching it.

In a live system each step leaves the system serving: units move over while the old structure drains, and no unit answers to both structures, the concern unity of command addresses. Compatibility is a contract at boundaries with clients who cannot change with you ([mechanisms and trusted boundaries](model.md#mechanisms-and-trusted-boundaries)). Inside a boundary where everything changes together, an adapter is usually only a cost, and the old structure goes in the cycle that builds its new owner.

Inherited decisions hold by their reasons, not their provenance. An old ontology has no right of inheritance, reopening a decision only because it differs is the opposite error, and reversing one's own ruling is fine when the reason is stated generally. Dead ends are evidence, and the direction of revision is recorded rather than a tidy path read backwards.

## Describing a design

A design is written for literal readers, many of them models. Requirements, contracts and invariants bind, a description of a mechanism records a current choice, and an explanation orients. Literal readers turn explanation into conditions and conditions into checks, so a design marks which is which. The FAA's order defines its verbs, "must" for mandatory, "should" for recommended, "may" for optional and "will" for futurity rather than a requirement ([FAA air traffic](cases/faa-air-traffic.md)), as RFC 2119 does for Internet standards. An explanation needed to make a requirement true is itself a requirement. Universal words are kept for invariants and for contracts that structure makes true, because non-provisions read as prohibitions and defensive wording reads as checks.

Each thing is stated once, where it is owned, as the FAA's letters of agreement "only supplement" the order and each covers one subject. One word per thing is fixed before several people write, and a word that two governing texts use differently is marked by source, as "support" is a command authority in joint doctrine and not in Army doctrine ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)). Conventions can tell a reader which statements ask and which report, as Kubernetes separates a spec, "a complete description of the desired state", from the status that reports on it ([Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md)). What two owners rely on is contract, what one relies on is mechanism, and a rule sits above the technology only if its arguments hold whatever the technology.

A good description says where it stops. The FAA order asks controllers to "exercise their best judgment" where it does not reach; joint doctrine calls the support relationship "a somewhat vague but very flexible arrangement" and puts the precision in each establishing directive; the end-to-end authors call their argument "a guideline", conditional on what the ends are. A lesson bound to one objective or technology names the assumptions it rests on and what would reverse it. Limits differ in kind, for instance not yet designed, absent from this build, accepted, unprobed or gated, and none is filled with an invented default; an element may be committed, a decision still to make with its alternatives, or something to establish against the real environment. A procedure can also omit what makes it work: Graban reads Toyota's 1992 booklet as describing rope and lamp, while what makes a pull feel cheap lives in the behaviour around them ([andon](cases/toyota-andon.md)).

Reasons, alternatives and history live in records beside the design. Completed records stay as written, a rewrite gets one change note, a decision record grown into a parallel account has become a second design, and direction from the holder of meaning is recorded verbatim and dated. The description leaves out history offered as a reason, restated platform semantics, coverage for its own sake and polish while the design still moves, and its entry document is an index. A reader who stops at any layer holds a correct system, and lower layers add rather than retell. Because the theory behind a design cannot be fully recovered from its artifacts, as Naur argued, questions and cases go beside its principles.

## Organizing the work

The effort that designs a system is itself an artificial system under one governance, with people and AI agents among its actors. The holder of meaning fixes what is asked, what the work is not to become and the premises held fixed, and the engineering judgment of others completes the rest. Delegation travels in briefs that give the task and why, what to read, the over-corrections to avoid, form and budget, the canonical names, and where to record objections. Coordination runs through a spine, one working document serving as the shared logical world (names, positions, withdrawn things with their carriers, budgets), and disagreement is written to it so that it does not become a second formulation.

Work is divided by responsibility rather than by directory, because the division reappears in what is built, as ownership of the shell decided where Multics put redirection; Conway's law states the general case. Writers get disjoint scopes and one synthesizer. Reading is divided by the system's boundaries and judging by cross-cutting question, with one judge who holds the whole and rulings that carry reasons. One independent cross-reading and one fix pass usually find what matters: contradictions between hands, removals without carriers, regrowth, unowned resources at joins. Style is not a finding, and a loop of review, fix and review tends to become the work. Only questions of meaning go up, each blocking only what depends on it.

Approving a plan adopts its defaults. A session is bounded to one question, phases that are expensive to undo are gated, and an intervention names a kind of growing structure rather than fixing instances. Plans, ledgers and review loops serve the work, authority lives in the deliverables, and state is recovered from the design and the product; a management layer that starts defining the work is goal displacement inside the team. As judgment, AI agents optimize whatever is checked, so an easily satisfied check yields false completion and instructions about what to verify get read as things to run. Their design work stays economical when they settle a problem by answering it rather than with a new abstraction, keep checks and ledgers in service of the work, fill a schema only as far as someone uses it, and keep a name that helped them think in the design's language. A build-cycle record says what was built, removed and left open, and is not evidence of verification; cycles that turn into audit sweeps are a reason to stop and report. Whether Conway's and Naur's observations carry over to AI agents without persistent memory is open.
