# Activities

Work on an artificial system moves among kinds of attention: saying what is required, deciding structure, building, running, finding out why a difficulty returns, changing what already runs, writing the design down, and organizing the people and AI agents who do this. The activities interleave and are not stages. Formulating and building change each other and neither finishes first: a draft design exposes a requirement nobody stated, and a requirement found late reorganizes the draft. A session goes better when it says which activity it is in, at what tempo and what will carry its result to the others, and on switching, what the previous activity was doing and why that is no longer the question. The division is a lens, like the model in [model.md](model.md), and each activity is described by what good work in it tends to look like. Where no case or source is named, the text states judgment from practice.

## Formulating what is required

Formulating names the stakeholders and what they require, the measures that bind and the indicators that inform, and the things and actions that matter in the system's own terms, and it decides what is left out. The governance the system is under settles what is being designed, so it is a natural place to start. An X-Road instance is what one governing authority administers, with its members as clients ([X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)), and a balancing authority works among generators and neighbours that are its environment ([grid](cases/grid-balancing.md)). Naming each outside organization with what the system relies on from it frames the work without testing whether the system qualifies, and where governance is partial the frame is used as far as it helps.

Requirements are best gathered in the words of those who rely on them, and they are usually few. SRE advises starting from "what your users care about, not what you can measure" and preferring "a loose target that you tighten" to current performance ([SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)). A property made a requirement because it is easy to check tends to drift toward stricter and costlier demands, and once anything is a requirement, debate turns to meeting it more cheaply. Asking which stakeholder would notice its absence, and whose failure it prevents, cuts both ways, since a measure users rely on binds like any requirement, with a bound that may be a ceiling as well as a minimum.

Stakeholders tend to state needs in the shape of the mechanism they expect, and separating the restriction a need implies from that mechanism leaves room for a better one. The end-to-end paper warns that a subsystem specified before its applications are known tempts its designer "to 'help' the users by taking on more function than necessary" ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)).

Much of a formulation was attached by an earlier solution, a neighbouring domain or a convenient representation. What an independent second implementation would need, and whether the structure would emerge if the system were built today, help sort it. Removing an attached element means naming what it was attached to, and one attachment usually carried several things (see [Evolving](#evolving)). An imported frame brings its hard problems, such as transactions and exactly-once delivery. Its lesson about observation is usually worth keeping and its promises only where a stakeholder needs them, as the datagram leaves loss visible and reliability to the ends that want it.

What is asked is a representation and can be wrong, so it is held loosely enough to change without losing the work done under it. It meets the world through the people who rely on it and through whatever will carry it out. A formulation stands on something when its parts can be named in fields that already exist and put to the people and things that would carry them; parts that make sense only against one another have begun to explain themselves. Agreement among its answers is a property of the representation, and a map of the problem space is a lens for covering ground, not a list of parts. The premises most often wrong are about what carries the system: the 737 MAX took one vane's reading as the angle of attack itself, and its assumption that crews would answer a trim runaway within seconds entered the hazard classification and the simulator method with no study behind it ([MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md)). Requirements that hold only under conditions no stakeholder has committed to have not yet touched the world, and an assumptions section reading "none" usually means nobody looked. Naming the premises nobody has looked at shows where a formulation is newest, and study of mature systems gives it footing.

A decided absence is stated once, with its cost, where the requirement it limits lives. An unreached one is left unstated, so that it hardens neither into a prohibition nor into a speculative mechanism. The order of building is not part of what the system is.

## Architecting

Architecting decides roles and actors, what is delegated and what is coordinated horizontally, layers and boundaries, how the state that matters comes to be and who may change it, and the mechanisms through which actors share what they know, coordinate and observe. It goes best from a draft design, a concrete arrangement a reader can argue with (actors with tasks, messages with fields, states with transitions), held as the reference questions are asked of rather than as a verdict. Inherited problems are restated plainly and asked of the draft, and one that lives nowhere in it came from the old form. Each is answered on its own, often in one of a few ways: the world dissolves it, an existing element carries it, it expands the design, it does not arise (and would under a condition worth naming), another owner holds it, or it lies outside. These are what answers usually look like, not a form to complete, and a way nobody needed leaves no gap. Inherited concepts are not mapped onto components one to one.

Under one governance, delegation and the horizontal relations of services, requests, allocation and support each get their own reasons, neither read off the other's shape and neither needing a planner over the whole, and the model gives the cases ([actors](model.md#actors)). Saying which structures a design has, and which it lacks, is part of the design. Roles defined elsewhere, in a standard, a regulation or an existing interface, are adopted where they fit rather than redefined, the design says how its governance gives each one authority, and an adopted role holds while the actors in it change.

Delegation is designed with what it carries, latitude written into the task, authority in stated amounts and a rule for reading what returns ([layers and delegation](model.md#layers-and-delegation)), and where each decision goes is asked of the draft, along with whether asking who decides removes an agreement protocol ([actors](model.md#actors)). A shared resource usually needs an allocator and a rule, and a constraint across actors a place where it takes effect, both of which trimming a design can remove unnoticed. For each piece of state that matters, the draft says how it comes to be: which actions change it, who answers for them, how copies are kept or derived, and whether the running system needs it as a thing of its own ([how things come to be](model.md#how-things-come-to-be-and-who-may-change-them)). Two places that can change one thing with nothing settling between them are work the design has not yet seen, and leaving one place, or putting a rule between them, settles it; neighbouring balancing authorities act on one agreed meter and so have no second reading to reconcile ([grid](cases/grid-balancing.md)). Boundaries tend to follow changes of ownership, execution, trust or lifetime.

A design leans on whatever carries it out, and choosing what to lean on is part of architecting. Each carrier works by its own field's principles, of computing, of organizations or of physics, and they bound the design: a balancing authority's first seconds belong to inertia and governors it builds on and does not design, and delegation by intent reaches as far as the trust, training and command climate of the people carrying it ([mission command](cases/mission-command.md)). The design says what it takes of each carrier and stops decomposing where it can take that as given ([where the two layers meet](model.md#where-the-two-layers-meet)).

Each layer and component is asked what it is for ([reading a design](model.md#reading-a-design)). Asking who would be worse off without an element, and what a user would notice if it were gone, finds the parts whose reason nobody can name, a good place to look when a design is heavy, though the question serves understanding and is no verdict. A mechanism meeting demand it was not built for can have its foundation widened, move to another layer, owner or moment, be absorbed by the world or an existing element, or retire with its carrier named, as Unix's uniform file interface, already used by every command, made the pipe possible. Who relies on the mechanism, whether the world carries it, who would notice its absence, where it has to live and whether it is one of a family are useful questions, roughly in that order, and any answer can end the inquiry.

## Realizing

Realizing builds, procures and reuses. It is where the design meets what will carry it, and construction is evidence about the formulation. Fields added to keep a concept alive, mirror records, mappings that preserve an identity and a second lifecycle beside a native one can mean unfinished code, and as often a design asking for what need not exist. The first reading adds objects and comes by default; the second removes work and has to be asked for. Either conclusion lands in the design. Evidence also returns through a question whose premise was examined, a flow traced until a step cannot be written, a survey of real use, and the places where effort gathers.

Real paths are traced in time order through every part they touch, failure and restart included, and a stop is modelled rather than explained away. The 737 MAX's simulator evaluation induced MCAS's stabilizer motion "regardless of underlying cause", so a wrong angle-of-attack reading arriving with stick shaker and alerts never reached a pilot in testing, and neither did the widened 2.5 degree authority ([MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md)). A reused part is asked again about the environment it now serves, and a reused practice about the people who will carry it: as Graban reports, workers at Toyota's Georgetown plant pulled the andon cord about 2,000 times a week against about twice at a Ford plant in Dearborn with similar hardware, and what differed was the response and how a pull was received ([andon](cases/toyota-andon.md)).

What runs first tends to become the architecture. Unix kept features "because they represented small, easily-coded changes to what existed", and growth by what is cheap can leave an accident that later gets a reason ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)). The judgment drawn here is that large coherent steps toward the final form usually serve better than placeholders or minimal versions, while pilots and staged rollouts answer questions about the world. A stub lets a missing owner look present, so an unbuilt capability is better left visibly unbuilt.

Construction takes definitions, a few requirements and a draft design rather than a list to realize item by item, and its unit of work is a change to the draft in the draft's terms. The frontier is the unbuilt parts whose prerequisites already work, and code that contradicts the design belongs to it. Mature comparators are evidence with a stated confidence, the behaviour of a system nobody has examined is not invented, and among comparable technologies the selection can rest on operating burden, familiarity and licence. Progress is what the product can now do, in final form and connected to the rest; counts of the formulation's own nouns, stubs, harnesses and renames are activity, and a cycle without progress calls for a change of approach.

## Operating and observing

Operating runs the feedback loops and keeps requirements met over time, each loop at a tempo matched to what it acts on ([feedback loops](model.md#feedback-loops-and-observation)), and many troubles in operation are decisions taken at the wrong tempo. Operation also keeps behaviour near the contract, since "users build on the reality of what you offer, rather than what you say you'll supply" ([SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)).

Indicators are read with what they indicate and revisited when they drift. A status that measures read and the work it stands for are changed by different means, and where the status can move alone, the number moves without the work. VHA's measures read consult status, so its directive bars closing consults by group update "because of the risk of closing a consult without delivering needed care" ([VistA consults](cases/vista-consults.md)). A measure, check or record that comes to stand for what is correct, beside the design and the product, can take the place of what the task is for, Merton's goal displacement, and be satisfied while the capability is absent ([what is required](model.md#artificial-systems-and-what-is-required-of-them)). The concern is a metric supplanting what the task is for, not measures as constraints on a design.

A check belongs to what it serves. One that tells builders their code does what they meant is part of building, one that users rely on is part of the product, and one where separately owned parts meet is part of the design; kept for any other reason, it is ceremony. A record shows what passed through whatever keeps it and nothing beyond: an andon board records a call, a response and a stop, not a defect fixed ([andon](cases/toyota-andon.md)), and X-Road's message records show what the exchange carried, not what then happened in the provider's system ([X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)). A filled schema or a settled status often answers a neighbouring question, and reading it as the answer to the real one takes the representation for the system.

Patterns in the work are observations too: effort gathering on one mechanism, a difficulty returning after repair, one cost dominating discussion. They show only to someone who holds the whole. Filling every gap has no stopping point of its own, and a sensible one is where going further prevents nobody's failure.

## Diagnosing and reformulating

A difficulty can be read three ways before it is repaired: as a capability the system lacks, as a design demanding what need not exist, or as a formulation producing the difficulty, in one question or in the whole problem. These are places to intervene, chosen by cost and by damage to what depends on them, with a running system's transition cost counted separately. The default reading is "unfinished", so the other two are asked for on purpose. If a much stronger capability would not change the judgment, the difficulty is not one of capability. Since difficulty is relative to a solver, changing the statement is a legitimate lever.

A question carries the premise of the structure that raised it, and the dissolving option is often among the choices with no reason given to take it. A procedure that picks among the options on the table cannot reach an answer above them: when every option couples two lifetimes, the question is whether the coupling should exist. Effort concentrated on one mechanism, or a requirement defended by measuring it better, points at the requirement rather than at a better mechanism. Other signs that the formulation is at fault include one tension under several vocabularies, one word naming two problems, a requirement no stakeholder relies on, an agreement problem where only one actor decides, a limit gathered at a join, and a term or status answering an adjacent question. GCCS-J's instruction makes the whole system the "system of record", which says where others are to take data from and nothing about how a track comes to be or what settles two theatre pictures that disagree, and a phrase that answers whom to read from tends to stop anyone asking the rest ([GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)). A sign says where to look and proves nothing, and it is repaired where it points rather than where it was noticed; a channel that keeps receiving the wrong kind of question needs a criterion on the channel. Where the whole formulation is suspect, asking who relies on the property, in their words, and what they would lose finds firm ground.

Pressure can be relieved by adding a capability, removing a representation or relinquishing a requirement, each hard to see from the one before, and asking which objects can go never reaches a bound. Revising a premise that a family of mechanisms shares is worth more than trimming each member, as the 737 MAX's premise about crew retrim shows ([reading a design](model.md#reading-a-design)).

Reformulation changes what is asked or how it is represented, and its forms are judged differently. A re-rendering asks the same question more cheaply and is judged by equivalence. A replacement answers a different question in the original's service and is judged by whether the answer transfers back: the end-to-end argument replaces the question of how reliable the network should be with the question of where the function can be completed and checked, whose answer holds whatever the network does and leaves reliability a question of performance ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)). A change of what is asked is judged by whether the stakeholders who rely on the answer take it up. Unix asked what a small group needed to program together on a cheap machine rather than what a general utility required, and the groups at Bell Laboratories who took it up were its judges, the Patent department "the first of many groups at the Laboratories to ratify our work" ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)). One change can take different forms for different stakeholders: read this way, SAP's derived balances are a re-rendering for those who read balances and a change for programs written against the old tables ([SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)). Misnaming the form claims an equivalence that does not hold, or skips a question the change could have answered.

A reformulation can lose part of the original, so it is held against what the original demanded, naming what the removed thing silently supplied: Unix gave up named shared segments, dynamic linking, user-visible locks and accounting. Too broad a reformulation cannot be acted on, and too narrow treats symptoms. The moves are open, and common ones fold a freedom into a definition, separate fused roles, dissolve a construct into a relation, move a choice to the layer that meets it, replace a resident controller with a decision re-made over durable state, widen, relax, restate the requirements, move a question to its owner, remove a decider, keep the observation and drop the delivery promise, or demote a specification to a map. A question moved to an owner moves nothing unless the owner exists and can use it, a property moved into a producer's precondition becomes an exposure nobody watches, and a representation that replaces a removed one needs the same scrutiny, so a better ontology is not by itself the answer.

Reformulation is not error correction. The earlier formulation was what the work needed, its products are kept as material, and a large model can be demoted to a map without being rewritten. A short record where the decision is made usually says what premise changed, what falls away, what survives and where, what weakened and what the original demanded, and the form the change takes for each stakeholder; earlier records stay as written. A change without a changed premise tends to be a patch, and one without a record a rename.

Escalation follows consequence for meaning, not difficulty or disagreement. Engineering choices, consequences of the design and questions research can answer are settled by whoever does the work, and a choice of meaning goes to its holder with the options, their costs, what it blocks and a default under which work continues; a lone designer still marks such choices. Before escalating or answering, it is worth asking whether what the question assumes should exist.

## Evolving

A running system changes best at its premises. Mechanisms come in families that share one, and revising the premise removes the family and explains each member, while trimming members one by one leaves the premise to grow them back. SAP's totals, indexes, update steps and reconciliation all rested on the cost of summing on disk, and when in-memory columns removed that cost the premise was revised once ([SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)). The affected part is then rewritten, not deleted clause by clause.

A removal states what still has to hold, what carries it now, what silently relied on the removed thing and what a user loses; "nothing", for something relied on, is the warning, and a removal that would reopen an irreversible effect on a real resource is worth refusing. Removed things regrow under new names, as smaller versions or by moving, and a rename is not a revision; a changed premise prevents regrowth, and a list of withdrawn things with their likely disguises catches it. Over-defence adds mechanisms, over-trimming silently leaves a real resource outside every rule, and losses gather at the joins with real resources. Removals are judged against what has to hold between stakeholders, over time and after loss, and a revision says enough when someone who did not make it can read it and find no reason to put things back.

Invariants are written with the failure each prevents, as Unix gives its rule that each directory appears in exactly one other "to simplify the writing of programs which visit subtrees" ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)), and restated in each revision rather than referred to. A reformulation that touches an invariant makes a new system and is better said to. As judgment, invariants are settled first under a narrow space of action, which is then widened without reopening them.

Widening a foundation tends to serve better than adding special cases, and a widening is measured by what the system can then express. Special mechanisms wait for need, while foundations that keep change cheap do not: the datagram's thin contract, "the minimum network service assumption", let one protocol carry a reliable stream and real-time speech over networks from satellite to local area nets ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)). An absence from today's build is not a prohibition, and policy kept apart from its mechanism changes without touching it.

In a live system each step leaves the system serving: units move over while the old structure drains, and no unit answers to both structures, the concern unity of command addresses. Compatibility is a contract at boundaries with clients who cannot change with you ([where the two layers meet](model.md#where-the-two-layers-meet)). Inside a boundary where everything changes together, an adapter is usually only a cost, and the old structure goes in the cycle that builds its new owner.

Inherited decisions hold by their reasons, not their provenance. An old ontology has no right of inheritance, reopening a decision only because it differs is the opposite error, and reversing one's own ruling is fine when the reason is stated generally. Dead ends are evidence, and the direction of revision is recorded rather than a tidy path read backwards.

## Describing a design

A design is written for people and AI agents who will act on it. They need to tell what is asked of them from what records a current choice and what explains, and they need the reasons, since a reader who has them can act where the text runs out, as a commander's intent "succinctly describes what constitutes success for the operation" so that a subordinate can act "when existing orders no longer fit the situation" ([mission command](cases/mission-command.md)). Readers, models especially, take explanation as instruction and instruction as something to check, so a design says plainly which of its statements ask. A text that many people act on can fix its verbs, as the FAA's order reads "must" as mandatory, "should" as recommended, "may" as optional and "will" as futurity rather than a requirement ([FAA air traffic](cases/faa-air-traffic.md)); most designs are served by sentences that say what they mean. An explanation without which a requirement cannot be met is part of the requirement. "Always", "never" and "every" are kept for what holds everywhere, such as an invariant or a contract the structure keeps, because a reader builds a check for each, and in the same way a sentence about what the design does not provide reads as a prohibition and defensive wording as a list of checks.

Each thing is stated once, where it is owned, as the FAA's letters of agreement "only supplement" the order and each covers one subject. Several writers agree early on the names where two meanings would build two different things, and leave the rest of the language plain. A design keeps the words of the field that will carry it, and a word that two governing texts use differently is marked by source, as "support" is a command authority in joint doctrine and not in Army doctrine ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)). Conventions can tell a reader which statements ask and which report, as Kubernetes separates a spec, "a complete description of the desired state", from the status that reports on it ([Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md)). What two owners rely on is contract, and what one relies on is mechanism. A statement belongs to the design in its own terms only if its reasons hold whatever carries it out, and one whose reasons belong to a technology or an organization says which. A description that tries to fix every word and settle every reading in advance, with defined terms, rule tables and markers of what binds, is read as a form to comply with, and its readers start conforming to the text instead of thinking about the system.

A good description says where it stops. The FAA order asks controllers to "exercise their best judgment" where it does not reach; joint doctrine calls the support relationship "a somewhat vague but very flexible arrangement" and puts the precision in each establishing directive; the end-to-end authors call their argument "a guideline", conditional on what the ends are. A lesson bound to one objective or technology names the assumptions it rests on and what would reverse it. Limits differ in kind, for instance not yet designed, absent from this build, accepted, not yet looked into or gated, and none is filled with an invented default; an element may be committed, a decision still to make with its alternatives, or something to find out from the real environment. A procedure can also omit what makes it work: Graban reads Toyota's 1992 booklet as describing rope and lamp, while what makes a pull feel cheap lives in the behaviour around them ([andon](cases/toyota-andon.md)).

Reasons, alternatives and history live in records beside the design. Completed records stay as written, a rewrite gets one change note, a decision record grown into a parallel account has become a second design, and direction from the holder of meaning is kept in their own words and dated. The description leaves out history offered as a reason, restated platform semantics, coverage for its own sake and polish while the design still moves, and its entry document is an index. A reader who stops at any layer holds the whole system at that depth, and lower layers add rather than retell. Because the theory behind a design cannot be fully recovered from its artifacts, as Naur argued, questions and cases go beside its principles.

## Organizing the work

The effort that designs a system is itself an artificial system under one governance, with people and AI agents among its actors. The holder of meaning fixes what is asked, what the work is not to become and the premises held fixed, and the engineering judgment of others completes the rest. Delegation travels in briefs that give the task and why, what to read, the over-corrections to avoid, form and budget, the names that matter and where to raise objections; like a commander's intent, a brief says what success is and leaves the means to whoever does the work. Coordination runs through one shared working document (the holder's direction in their own words, names in use, positions taken, withdrawn things with what now carries them, budgets), and disagreement is written to it so that it does not become a second formulation. Such a document guides thinking. Grown into a specification of terms and rules to enforce, it turns writers toward conforming and becomes a second design that the real one is made to answer to.

Work is divided by responsibility rather than by directory, because the division reappears in what is built. Multics's I/O system and its shell were built at different sites, and Ritchie recalls, "We didn't consider making changes to the shell (it was their program)", so redirection stayed a separate command ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)); Conway's law states the general case. Writers get disjoint scopes and one person who brings the parts together. Reading is divided by the system's boundaries, and one reader who holds the whole looks across the parts for what no single writer can see, such as contradictions between hands, removals with nothing carrying what they did, regrowth and resources at joins that nobody owns, and gives a reason for each change asked. One such reading and one pass of changes usually find what matters. Style is not what the reading is for, and a loop of review, fix and review tends to become the work. Only questions of meaning go up, each blocking only what depends on it.

Approving a plan adopts its defaults. A session is bounded to one question, phases that are expensive to undo are gated, and an intervention names a kind of growing structure rather than fixing instances. Plans, ledgers and review loops serve the work. The work is the design and the product, state is recovered from them, and a management layer that starts defining the work is goal displacement inside the team. As judgment, AI agents optimize whatever is checked, so an easily satisfied check yields false completion and instructions about what to verify get read as things to run. Their design work stays economical when they settle a problem by answering it rather than with a new abstraction, keep checks and ledgers in service of the work, fill a schema only as far as someone uses it, and let a term coined while thinking go once the thought can be said in words the design already uses. Left to themselves they drift toward closed vocabularies, and every coined term a design keeps is one more thing each later reader must learn. A record of a build cycle says what was built, removed and left open, describing the work without vouching for it, and cycles that turn into sweeps for things to fix are a reason to stop and report. Whether Conway's and Naur's observations carry over to AI agents without persistent memory is open.
