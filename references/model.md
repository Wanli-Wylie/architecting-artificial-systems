Each part of this model is a way of understanding, with its uses, usual reasons and limits. Claims rest on the cases or are marked as judgment, and the cases show where the model adds nothing.

## Artificial systems and what is required of them

Stakeholders' needs reach a design as requirements, in their terms, with a scope and a bound. Requirements are usually few beside the means that meet them, and those that shape an architecture most are often qualities such as latency, availability, precision or security; the DARPA Internet ranked survivability first, one reason it kept a conversation's state in the hosts ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)). Cost, responsiveness and data protection enter the same way, as what some stakeholder requires rather than as aims of their own. Without the few requirements, mechanisms come to justify one another.

A bound can be a ceiling as well as a minimum. SRE treats an availability target as "both a minimum and a maximum" and takes Chubby down on purpose when it has run too well ([SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)). Allocated to a part, a requirement becomes part of its contract, as an objective does for a service and its clients.

A measure can be a requirement that binds construction, such as an availability objective or NERC's CPS1, or an indicator of something it captures in part, read with what it indicates so that it does not take the place of what the task is for (Merton's goal displacement, Goodhart's law). NERC replaced CPS2 once it "did not provide the correct signal for maintaining frequency" ([grid](cases/grid-balancing.md)), and VHA, whose measures read consult status, bans closing consults by group update and requires a real status change rather than a comment ([VistA consults](cases/vista-consults.md)). GCCS-J's availability figure reaches the picture's quality only through someone classing a degraded picture as an outage ([GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)). A classification can stand in for what it assesses, as MCAS's hazard classification set the function's analysis and came to stand for the airplane's real tolerance of the fault ([MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md)).

Kept apart from its means, a requirement lets the means change, as SRE separates an indicator's specification from its implementation; fused, a means hardens into a promise nobody asked for, while the reverse error demotes a relied-on requirement to an indicator. A requirement naming a mechanism may be a real need, as Estonian regulation names the trust services a member's e-seal must use ([X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)), or a promoted means, and who relies on it tells which. In judgment, a requirement stakeholders rely on stays strict however costly: a thin contract is a choice about what its clients need, as the datagram suits ends that check for themselves, not an economy to apply generally. A weakened or withdrawn requirement is named where it was stated, or it gets built as if it still held.

Some requirements come from beyond the governance boundary, as a balancing authority meets NERC's measures of its share in interconnection frequency while the standards and their revision stay with NERC. Meaning held in a statute, contract, standard or directive has a holder and a path for its changes, which a design names. A requirement can be served without being enforced: X-Road serves Estonia's once-only principle by making a request to the data's owner cheap, and nothing in it prevents a second copy.

## Three perspectives on one system

The logical, actor and implementation perspectives describe one system and are refined against each other. The logical world directs, the implementation constrains, and an existing capability can be lifted into a logical action, as a Foundry action type wraps a call to an outside system ([Palantir](cases/palantir-ontology.md)). They are not strata: looked into, a capability an actor uses can turn out to be actors, as the unit a commander tasks contains commanders ([mission command](cases/mission-command.md)). A specification lives in the world: what a mechanism is specified to do, together with what holds of the world regardless, is meant to give what is wanted, and where the world is software, it is the existing stacks.

Within the actor perspective, actors under one governance often organize in two ways at once: vertically by delegation, and horizontally by services and requests, allocation, shared information and arrangements such as joint doctrine's support relationships. The two need not share a shape. Joint doctrine runs operational command to the combatant commanders and administrative control through the Military Departments, so a Service component command answers to both ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)). FAA facilities answer to their service areas while the Command Center directs traffic management through units inside them ([FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md)). [VHA](cases/vista-consults.md)'s line settles who must comply and with what resources, while consults move between services as requests the line does not carry. Borg's priority bands rank kinds of work, not teams, and its quota rules apply to every team alike ([Borg](cases/borg.md)).

Many systems show only one structure or neither. Palantir's documentation describes neither, nor do SAP's sources ([SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)); the [end-to-end argument](cases/end-to-end.md) and Multics and [Unix](cases/multics-unix.md) concern layers of realization; [andon](cases/toyota-andon.md) and [SRE](cases/sre-slos.md) are mostly horizontal; [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md) has no delegation across its members. Other structures run beside these, such as containment, code layout and the hierarchies of realization described under layers. Where two touch, they tend to meet at a named point rather than by matching shape, as the FAA's order separates traffic management initiatives from "controller coordinated actions" at one controller's position. A source tree, in judgment, follows what changes together rather than the logical world.

## The logical world

The logical world holds objects, relations, states and actions, each action with its intent, preconditions, effect, completion condition and reported result. Observed fact, estimate, intent and permission stay distinct in it; an estimate has a source and an uncertainty, is read by choices and is usually kept out of definitions, and a sample is not a commitment. Schemas, types and rules are optional means of expressing it, and where a running system records them they give designers and users a common language, as [Foundry](cases/palantir-ontology.md)'s object types and the spec and status of [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) objects do. Whatever carries it, information systems most often, is a mechanism with its own owners, contracts and boundaries, and represents the logical world without being it.

A definition says what it fixes and what it leaves free, and latitude belongs in it, as [mission command](cases/mission-command.md)'s intent "defines the limits within which subordinates may exercise initiative" (ADRP 6-0, 2012) and the [FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md) writes traffic management units' latitude into their authority. Latitude granted separately has an edge someone must police and tends to become a second decider. In judgment, a restriction earns its place by what a realizer can derive from it, a construct belongs where the realizer must see it to choose well, and what no requirement, contract or invariant fixes is the realizer's choice, said once. Nothing from one realization flows up into the meaning, so legality does not depend on what can execute today, and nothing in the meaning removes a realizer's real choice, as the datagram contract leaves networks free in how they deliver. A matter is usually bound when it becomes known, earlier where a commitment buys something, as quota held for months lets a [Borg](cases/borg.md) user expect production work to run.

A fact's source of truth sits with whoever has authority over it, a separate question from storage. Kubernetes replicates objects and reads them through caches while recording which manager owns each field; Foundry lets each datasource set whether a user's edit or the feed wins; [GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)'s instruction names the whole system the "system of record" and no source per track, a gap the term makes visible. What creates work is a redundant source of truth, two places each authoritative for one fact with no rule between them, while a check between two independent sources compares two facts. Integration across owners usually leaves the source where it is: "X-Road does not change ownership of data", and an inter-facility consult's "results are filed at the resulting facility".

What can be derived from definitions and readable facts is usually derived, and, in judgment, what needs storing is mostly what crosses a boundary: durable progress, work in flight, adopted choices not yet carried out, facts only a remote system knows. A stored derived form is a performance choice with a consistency cost and a premise that can expire, a second copy that every event must update. [SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)'s classic ledger stored totals beside line items because summing on disk was slow, and dropped them once in-memory storage removed the reason. Kubernetes stores no workflow: the plan is state, so a restarted controller "simply picks up where it left off", entry, takeover and a fresh start are one path, and each status records the generation of the spec it reflects.

In judgment, work is best named by a canonical description that holds exactly what changes the obligation, and nothing about how it is being solved, so that sharing follows from identity and demand forms a graph; a name that omits an input of the answer makes reuse silently wrong. Sharing uses what exists, then waits on what is in flight, then proposes anew, with a repair path where identity appears only during solving.

A distinction usually earns its place where someone acts on it differently or where audit or regulation needs it, as each of [VistA](cases/vista-consults.md)'s consult statuses changes what a service or a measure does; finer detail serves people better as text. In judgment, a kind that reaches an interface as a closed enumeration makes any addition a breaking change, and is better a value looked up, with "not supported in this version" a valid answer. A design concept need not exist at runtime unless the running system does something with it, and it usually earns runtime identity where it crosses a boundary (a process, a restart, concurrent owners, asynchronous completion, untrusted input, a public contract); a useful test is which behaviours become inexpressible without it. A system may also reify part of its logical world on purpose, as Foundry's Ontology gives people and programs shared objects and actions to query and act on. That is more than passing messages, and it costs a second edit path, a queue, a merge rule and write-back to source systems.

## Actors

An actor's autonomy is set by the decision problem it actually holds, and a stable interface lets its inside change. The description fits where a unit chooses among what its task leaves open: a [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md)'s dispatchers choose which resources to move, while its governors respond by droop setting and its generation control by a fixed law. A shell interpreting commands by fixed rules is a mechanism, and its users are the actors. Often a source's role names do the work and "actor" adds nothing, as with the [FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md)'s controllers or [VHA](cases/vista-consults.md)'s clinical services, and calling an andon pull a decision overstates a trained response ([andon](cases/toyota-andon.md)). An actor typically takes part in both structures at once: an air traffic sector works its traffic under its facility while handing aircraft to neighbours and taking restrictions from traffic management, and a clinical service answers consults while sending its own.

Roles come from a system's own design and from professional norms, standards, laws, doctrine, contracts and existing interfaces, and the governance decides how each acquires authority and responsibility in the system. [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)'s service client and service provider are the standard's roles, given authority by the centre's admission of a member and each provider's grant of access, neither of them delegation. The pilot in command's role is set by regulation the FAA's orders do not change. One actor can fill several roles, as US Strategic Command fills three for [GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md), and a role can pass between actors.

Designs align what an actor may decide with what it can bring about, by specification and over time, as [mission command](cases/mission-command.md) pays for initiative in advance through training. Authority given to whoever holds the information works when a response stands behind it: an andon pull needs a team leader who comes, and [SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)'s error budget "relies on an SRE team having the authority to actually stop launches". Authority and responsibility can part, as FM 3-0 lets commanders delegate authority but not "absolve themselves of the responsibility".

A policy settles choices a mechanism or task leaves open, kept apart from the mechanism so either can change. X-Road's security policy, its trusted certification authorities and parameters such as the OCSP response lifetime, differs between instances without changing verification. Elsewhere the split is a reading: [Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)'s per-user shell is policy in a later gloss, SRE's "error budget policy" a whole approved document, and command in mission command a judgment not reduced to a rule. In judgment, varying only what is meant to be control shows whether a choice is open: if a result the definition should fix then changes, the repair belongs in the definition, not in a rule of the planner, and much of what a domain calls a decision is a derivation.

An extension point earns its cost when a policy will go there or a platform is built for independent evolution, as [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) admits operators for custom resources. A proposal is checked against the facts current when it returns, as [Borg](cases/borg.md)'s master refuses the scheduler's assignments when they rest on out-of-date state, and however capable the proposer, a proposal does not replace the authority that admits it: [Foundry](cases/palantir-ontology.md)'s AI agents act within scopes inherited from a user or a project. In judgment, where choices are made by improving policies, as optimizers and AI agents make them, the system owes them the conditions for good choice (what can be expressed, what switching costs, what is knowable when choosing) and the facts the policies read, rather than a score to raise.

Decisions generally go best where the information and the ability to change the outcome meet, before the irreversible step, and are centralized where consistency, fairness, accountability or scale outweigh local knowledge; placing them by symmetry tends to get this wrong (judgment). Separation stays with the controller who holds the airspace, while the Command Center approves interfacility traffic management initiatives. Mission command moves decisions to "the point of action" and keeps precise control where synchronization is critical, and Borg schedules each cell centrally, rotating among users "to ensure fairness". Asking who decides often removes the need for an agreement protocol: Kubernetes records which manager owns each field and treats another manager's change as a conflict, so a user hands the replica count to the autoscaler rather than fight it, and [joint doctrine](cases/joint-command-support.md) lets no two commanders hold the same command relationship over one force at once.

## Feedback loops and observation

Loops run at several tempos. A [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md)'s balance is held by inertia in seconds, governors over tens of seconds, generation control acting on area control error every few seconds and dispatchers over minutes, in timeframes that overlap, and the bias term keeps the slower loop from withdrawing the frequency support the faster one gives. A design aligns update rates, action times and decision frequency, and says what may happen while information is insufficient. In judgment, revision has tempos too: adaptation at each event, policies between runs, definitions deliberately and with a record.

Observation is organized around the decisions it serves, a fact belonging where it can change the legality, cost or future value of an available action, as [mission command](cases/mission-command.md) ties critical information requirements to decision points. Much of it reaches several layers through shared records and displays, such as area control error, an [andon](cases/toyota-andon.md) board or a common operational picture, which also help actors understand one another.

An action's intent, acceptance, progress and outcome are distinct observations: a [VHA](cases/vista-consults.md) consult is Pending when ordered, Active once the receiving service receives it, Partial Results while the reply is unsigned and Complete when it is signed. Completion rests on the effect or on confirmation by whatever carried it out, and in [Foundry](cases/palantir-ontology.md) the choice between a writeback webhook, which aborts the edits on failure, and a side-effect webhook, which shows no failure, decides what a success message reports. [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)'s log proves what an exchange carried and, in judgment, not what the provider did.

An unknown outcome is usually kept unknown and observed again, with the uncertainty on the thing it concerns, as [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) marks a quiet node Unknown and waits five minutes by default before evicting. Silence can end a wait by policy, as that eviction or [GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)'s calls to a site silent for ten minutes do, and it settles an outcome only where a protocol gives it that meaning, as a two-phase commit coordinator aborts on a missing vote. In judgment, waiting is an answer that names the facts that would change it, and how long to wait is a policy on an existing mechanism, as the eviction delay is, rather than states of its own. Estimates are kept apart from commitments: [Borg](cases/borg.md) places production work against its limits and other work against its reservation, an estimate that tracks actual use, and a machine that runs short sheds the lowest priority first. Higher-layer observations are derived by stated rules with a path back to the detail, as area control error is a formula over telemetry; where a source states none, as with GCCS-J's "fusion", naming the gap is the use.

## Layers and delegation

Hierarchy has several sources: an interface implemented beneath an abstraction, as [Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)'s system calls offer processes and files; a function realized by assembled parts, as [GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)'s global picture is fused from theatre pictures; execution spread across units, as in a [Borg](cases/borg.md) cell; control built over physical dynamics, from a [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md)'s controls to [MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md) beneath the pilot; and delegation, the vertical structure among actors. A source's "level", "echelon" or "layer" stays its own, as [Palantir](cases/palantir-ontology.md)'s "operational layer" and NERC's continuum do.

Layers have many reasons, each buying something: hiding a decision likely to change (Parnas), as making the shell an ordinary program let the command language change without the system; parallelism; load distribution; fault isolation, as small [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) controllers fail alone; scale; responsiveness; independent evolution; division of responsibility; decisions placed near local information. A higher layer often could decide as well and delegates anyway, as [mission command](cases/mission-command.md) does because information sits where the action is. A layer usually costs an interface, a failure mode and latency, and a function copied below costs more, since applications that do not need it pay and the lower layer knows less. The [end-to-end argument](cases/end-to-end.md) places a function where the requirement and the knowledge to complete and check it coincide, "not an absolute rule, but rather a guideline". Simon's near-decomposability explains why a layer can be used through its contract alone, and why not where interactions across its boundary are strong.

Delegation turns one of an actor's actions into tasks for others, with latitude, inherited authority, constraints and an expected result, and reads what returns by a composition rule that asks more than every subtask having ended. Mission command's intent "describes what constitutes success", so a unit can be lost without the larger task becoming undefined. In Kubernetes the task travels as desired state and returns as status, and a Deployment is complete when its replicas are updated and available, whatever its ReplicaSet reports. [Joint doctrine](cases/joint-command-support.md) grades the authority delegation carries: combatant command "cannot be delegated", operational and tactical control are bounded parts of it, and "forces, not command relationships, are transferred". Results are abstracted upward, and capability and deviations are reported back. Where delegates delegate in turn, complete actors compose into larger ones, a unit acting as one actor for its higher command, with no permanent centre aggregating the whole, and the recursion stops at a trusted boundary.

The recursive form fits parts of one kind that are nearly independent. It strains where parts run jointly, where the best parts do not make the best whole, where the choice among a few large realizations matters, or where a step is a one-shot effect; mission command meets units that are not independent with control measures and Army support relationships, not more delegation. In judgment, a task splits only as far as its results can be put back together, and coupling is stated, since parts exchanging intermediate results are one joint execution. Steps are not transactions: where one is refused after another has taken effect, both stand and the next derivation finds what remains, and admitting each step against current facts usually serves better than proving feasibility over the whole. Recursion in what is asked, in a method and in the runtime are different things, and most errors here report a property of the method or the runtime as one of the problem. Recursion alone is no reason for a process, owner or registry per layer, and capacity delegated down a tree fuses responsibility with resources. Where decomposition stops is a choice, opaque by decision differing from not yet looked at, and moving it changes the representation.

Delegation does not say how actors in different branches work together, and horizontal relations are not a gap in it. Sources use "delegate" more widely: a letter of agreement can "Delegate responsibility for control of IFR aircraft" to another facility, a horizontal transfer of airspace with no latitude or expected result. The model keeps the word for the vertical form. The FAA's delegation of certification work to Boeing's staff is that form, and what came back was documents not updated as MCAS grew.

Where layers act on one thing, each usually needs to know what the other may do. MCAS's authority over the stabilizer grew from 0.6 to 2.5 degrees while the manuals, training and safety assessment stayed behind, and its composition with the pilot rested on an assumed reaction time rather than a stated rule; naming such an assumption is the use of the composition rule.

## Horizontal coordination

Under one governance, actors coordinate across the delegation structure by several means at once, none needing a global planner, and requests under contracts are the commonest. An [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md) member calls another's service under an access agreement, checked by the provider's security server; a [VHA](cases/vista-consults.md) service's Receive action "puts your clinic on record as accepting responsibility for completing the consult"; a controller hands an aircraft to the next sector at a point a letter of agreement fixes, and the receiver may set restrictions before accepting. The provider decides how to meet a request, which is not a delegated task even between services in one line of management, since the responsibility comes from the receiver's own act and the agreements.

Allocation divides a shared resource under a rule an allocator enforces. [Borg](cases/borg.md) keeps four things apart: priority ranks kinds of work, quota bounds what a user may ask for at a priority, admission control rejects a job its quota does not cover, and the scheduler places admitted work. Quota itself is granted and priced in capacity planning, outside Borg. Allocation within one governance can thus use price-like signals set outside the enforcing system, and a rule can be limited to stop a failure mode, as production tasks may not preempt one another, to avoid cascades. A constraint spanning actors usually needs a place of enforcement, and coupling carried as a fact at each part's boundary often replaces negotiation, as a [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md) acts on its own area control error, computed from scheduled interchange and a meter agreed with each neighbour.

Shared records can carry a process's meaning rather than messages about it, as a VHA consult "acts as a depository of information about itself" and [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) loops with no delegation between them coordinate through objects they read and write.

Support relationships are set by a common superior. [Joint doctrine](cases/joint-command-support.md) has an establishing directive state purpose, resources, priority and the supported commander's degree of authority, leaves "the forces, tactics, methods, procedures, and communications" to the supporting commander, and makes the establishing authority "responsible for determining a solution" when needs cannot be met. Army doctrine's support relationships differ, so the word is used with its doctrine named.

Agreements among units are another means. [FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md) letters of agreement cover procedures needing more than one facility's concurrence and "only supplement" the national order; some reach an airport manager or another agency, so a contract can span the governance boundary. VHA's service agreements are written by the services and signed by their chiefs. Neither is a market.

The governance usually contributes a place for what actors cannot settle: FAA facilities take unresolved problems to higher management, [SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)'s example policy sends error budget disputes to the CTO, and a commander with joint coordinating authority can require consultation but "does not have the authority to compel agreement", so where "essential agreement cannot be obtained, the matter shall be referred to the appointing authority" ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)). A design can also give conflicting interests a settled place, as SRE's error budget lets development and operations settle release pace by one agreed number, measured by a monitoring system neither controls.

Boundaries tend to fall where ownership, execution or trust change, because each costs a protocol, a failure mode and a hand-off of state that those places need anyway. Conway's law describes organizations' boundaries reappearing in what they build, as Multics's I/O system and shell, built at different sites, kept redirection a separate command. In a system assembled from existing parts, much of its own work lies between them and goes unowned if the parts are assumed to compose by themselves. A join works when it has its own meaning and owner. X-Road gives exchange among members signed, time-stamped, logged calls, with a central server holding participation and nothing about citizens, and [Foundry](cases/palantir-ontology.md) hosts part of the logical world over separately kept records. Boeing's engineering and manual writers, the FAA's Flight Standardization Board and the operators depended on one another's documents, and the omission of [MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md) travelled along that dependence, which no delegation carried. In judgment, limits in a mature design gather at joins and are better met by giving the join meaning with one owner than by enlarging either side. Each mechanism for a join is one approach among several, and a small system may need none beyond its program types. In judgment too, a shared abstraction over differing mechanisms is tested against each instance it claims to cover, since who performs an act follows from who owns it, and a second classification axis has to be kept up everywhere it applies.

## Mechanisms and trusted boundaries

Every action has a real bearer, and a designer first asks what the world already carries, as a [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md) builds on its units' inertia and governors. In judgment, operating systems, services, professions, procedures and physical processes have semantics and lifecycles a rebuild would copy worse, though coordinating them may be the system's own job, and a survey of what they expose tests a structure rather than supplying one.

Decomposition stops at trusted boundaries, where a unit is taken as behaving by its contract, with outcomes including failure, partial results and "not yet known"; those outcomes are the design's real assumption about the unit. [MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md) took its single angle-of-attack sensor as given, with no "wrong" among its outcomes, though the fleet had seen 25 stick-shaker activations around takeoff in 17 years. The datagram contract names loss, reordering and duplication, and the application checks end to end what it relies on, the complement of a thin contract. Trust can be social, as on early [Unix](cases/multics-unix.md) without memory protection.

Structure that makes a property true usually costs less than checking it, and a check catches only what its author imagined; Unix lets each directory appear in exactly one other, protecting what programs walking the tree rely on. Checks earn their place where separately owned or trusted parts meet, or where a wrong claim would silently corrupt something: at MIT, per-hop checksums covered the wire and not the gateways' buffers, and a gateway swapping byte pairs corrupted source files. In judgment, parts built and released together usually trust each other's outputs, so a mismatch should fail loudly, and removing a validation does not remove its precondition. Compatibility with clients who cannot change with you is a contract paid at the boundary, as [SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)'s compatibility views keep old programs running "without changes" at a stated cost in performance.

A few invariants, such as that a resource may be reused or that an effect did not happen, need positive evidence in every interleaving, which a timeout supplies only inside a protocol built for it, such as a lease with fencing tokens (judgment). Loss is easiest to reason about along one path, resuming from what is durable and freshly observable, as a restarted [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) controller re-reads the store and fate-sharing loses a conversation's state only with its host; extra recovery paths tend to interact and hide faults. The [end-to-end paper](cases/end-to-end.md) keeps the observation and drops the delivery promise: acknowledgement and duplicate suppression belong to the application, which wants "I did it" or "I didn't". A repeat absorbed by name is harmless, a second real effect is not, and compensation belongs to the effect's owner.

In judgment, lifecycles belong to the mechanisms that have them, and nothing is reused while another actor may still reach it. Bounds belong where a real resource requires them, as [Borg](cases/borg.md) limits production quota to a cell's capacity, or where a restart would not converge, and a bound on something that is not a resource tends to manufacture overflow protocols. What a mechanism caches, and for how long, sets what outage it survives, as the validity of [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)'s cached configuration bounds how long the central server may be down. A reused mechanism is asked again about its new environment, as MCAS extended the NG's speed trim system into a new airframe.

## Correspondence and economy

A design can be read from the requirements down: each important requirement has an actor able to meet it, each decision its information and authority, each action a way to happen and to be seen to have happened, each claimed result an observational basis, and each constraint across actors a place of enforcement. The [andon](cases/toyota-andon.md) shows them together: someone at the point of work able to signal, a lamp that shows it, a team leader who answers, and a fixed-position stop where the constraint across stations takes effect. [MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md) shows one missing: the crew held authority over the stabilizer without knowing MCAS existed.

A design is also read from its structure up, asking what each part is for, with open reasons: a requirement met, a boundary spanned, a decision informed, a failure contained, a load spread, a change made independent, legibility to those who run it. The question keeps designs economical: derived forms are derived, sources of truth are not duplicated, and a premise that a family of mechanisms serves is revised as one. [SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)'s change of one premise about cost removed a whole family; at Boeing one premise, that crews would retrim within seconds, served the hazard classification, the simulator method, the single sensor and the manuals, and was never tested against the case the single sensor made likely.

The readings check each other: built from requirements alone, a design adds an entity per correspondence, and trimmed from structure alone, it loses correspondences whose invariants were out of view. Every heuristic here has an over-correction, and tensions between them are settled by what the structure is for. In judgment, the aim is a more capable system with less management rather than a smaller one, since minimalism for its own sake pushes performance into special cases. A lower layer's need is better met by repairing the upper layer than by a patch below that hides the defect. Brooks's distinction sorts the rest: what is required, what crosses a boundary and what someone acts on are essential however inelegant.

A system of complete actors acts as one actor of a larger one and can change inside while its contracts, authority and timing hold. In judgment, attention moves from what is required to the logical world, to who decides and observes, to mechanisms, and back as each exposes gaps in another. A design can satisfy every correspondence and pass every reading and still be the wrong design, because the model represents the system and is not it.
