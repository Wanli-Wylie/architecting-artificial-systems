The model is a way of thinking about artificial systems, worked out through the cases. It looks at a system at two layers, what the system is in its own terms and what carries it out, and most of what follows is about the first, how its actors are organized, and where it meets the second. Its words are offered where they help, and a field's own words are kept where they serve better.

## Artificial systems and what is required of them

Stakeholders' needs reach a design as requirements, in their terms, with a scope and a bound. Requirements are usually few beside the means that meet them, and those that shape an architecture most are often qualities such as latency, availability, precision or security; the DARPA Internet ranked survivability first, one reason it kept a conversation's state in the hosts ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)). Cost, responsiveness and data protection enter the same way, as what some stakeholder requires rather than as aims of their own. Without the few requirements, mechanisms come to justify one another.

A bound can be a ceiling as well as a minimum. SRE treats an availability target as "both a minimum and a maximum" and takes Chubby down on purpose when it has run too well ([SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)). Allocated to a part, a requirement becomes part of its contract, as an objective does for a service and its clients.

A measure can be a requirement that binds construction, such as an availability objective or NERC's CPS1, or an indicator of something it captures in part, read with what it indicates so that it does not take the place of what the task is for (Merton's goal displacement, Goodhart's law). NERC replaced CPS2 once it "did not provide the correct signal for maintaining frequency" ([grid](cases/grid-balancing.md)), and VHA, whose measures read consult status, bans closing consults by group update and requires a real status change rather than a comment ([VistA consults](cases/vista-consults.md)). GCCS-J's availability figure reaches the picture's quality only through someone classing a degraded picture as an outage ([GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)). A classification can stand in for what it assesses, as MCAS's hazard classification set the function's analysis and came to stand for the airplane's real tolerance of the fault ([MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md)).

Kept apart from its means, a requirement lets the means change, as SRE separates an indicator's specification from its implementation; fused, a means hardens into a promise nobody asked for, while the reverse error demotes a relied-on requirement to an indicator. A requirement naming a mechanism may be a real need, as Estonian regulation names the trust services a member's e-seal must use ([X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)), or a promoted means, and who relies on it tells which. A requirement stakeholders rely on stays strict however costly: a thin contract is a choice about what its clients need, as the datagram suits ends that check for themselves, not an economy to apply generally. A weakened or withdrawn requirement is named where it was stated, or it gets built as if it still held.

Some requirements come from beyond the governance boundary, as a balancing authority meets NERC's measures of its share in interconnection frequency while the standards and their revision stay with NERC. Meaning held in a statute, contract, standard or directive has a holder and a path for its changes, which a design names. A requirement can be served without being enforced: X-Road serves Estonia's once-only principle by making a request to the data's owner cheap, and nothing in it prevents a second copy.

## Two layers of one system

A system can be looked at in two ways that belong together. At the metaphysical layer it is what it is in its own terms: the things it deals in and how they relate, what happens to them, who acts and who answers for what, how work is handed down and how it is coordinated across. At the physical layer it is whatever carries that out: programs, machines, networks and stores, devices and physical processes, and people and organizations as they actually behave. An air traffic facility has sectors, aircraft under a controller's control, handoffs and letters of agreement, and it also has radar, radio, screens and the controllers who work them ([FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md)).

The first layer is metaphysical in a plain sense: it says what the system takes there to be, and none of it can be read off what carries it. A consult's being Pending is nowhere in the disk blocks that hold its record, and a force's being under one commander's operational control is nowhere in the soldiers. Such things exist because people and programs treat them as existing and act on them, under an order that says what counts as what, which is how Searle described institutions: a signed reply counts as completing a consult ([VistA consults](cases/vista-consults.md)), radar contact as accepting a handoff, an establishing directive as making one commander the supporter of another ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)). They are no less real for being made, since people are bound and refused by them, but what is made could be made otherwise, and that is what lets it be designed.

What a thing is and who may act on it do not come apart. A consult is partly who may receive, forward, cancel and complete it; a Kubernetes object is partly which manager holds each of its fields; a mission is partly the intent that bounds what a subordinate may choose ([mission command](cases/mission-command.md)). Entities and relations, acts and actors, responsibilities and capabilities, organization and coordination are therefore one inquiry. Each field has its own words for them, a commander's intent, a controller's handoff, a field manager, a data owner, and the model's words, such as actor, role, task, delegation and request, serve where a field has none or two fields need one in common.

Each part of the physical layer works by the principles of its own field. Programs, stores and networks follow those of computer science: a copy can be stale when it is used, a message can be lost or delivered twice, a machine can go silent without saying whether it has failed. People and organizations follow those that management and organizational theory study: people weigh what a signal will cost them, shared understanding takes time to build, and a role whose value nobody sees is at risk of being cut ([andon](cases/toyota-andon.md)). Physical processes follow physics: rotating mass gives up energy as frequency falls, and a governor arrests the fall without returning frequency to nominal ([grid](cases/grid-balancing.md)). The model contains none of these principles and is agnostic about which of them realize a given system, but it never forgets that something does. Whatever the metaphysical layer describes happens only through something physical, and the principles of that thing limit what the design can be.

The metaphysical layer directs: it says what is wanted and what things mean, and physical means are chosen and arranged to carry it, or an existing capability is lifted into it, as a Foundry action type wraps a call to an outside system ([Palantir](cases/palantir-ontology.md)). The physical layer bounds: it settles what can be carried, how fast, at what cost and through which failures. SAP's classic ledger kept totals beside the line items because summing on disk was slow, and the constraint shaped the model itself, with totals tables, index tables, update steps inside every posting and reconciliation at period end; in-memory columns removed it, and the family went with it ([SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)). What a balance means did not change, only what the model had to contain. Meaning is not defined by what today's mechanism happens to do, and nothing in it removes a realizer's real choice, as the datagram contract leaves networks free in how they deliver ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)). What a design keeps, computes, checks and waits for is where the physical principles decide.

People and organizations appear at both layers. In doctrine a commander holds a mission, authority in stated grades and a responsibility that delegating does not remove. Whether subordinates then choose as the commander would depends on whether they hold the commander's reasons, which training, dialogue and shared experience build over time. Trust, training and command climate are the physical side of delegation by intent, and a force whose commanders have not trained together has the same orders on paper and a different force in the field.

The two layers are not two more steps in a hierarchy, since every step has both: Unix's files and processes are what programs deal in, and code, tables and the machine carry them ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)), and the unit a commander tasks contains commanders. A layer on its own here means such a step ([layers and delegation](#layers-and-delegation)). A specification is met in the world: what a mechanism is specified to do, together with what holds of its surroundings regardless, is meant to give what is wanted, and where the surroundings are software, they are the stacks already running.

Because the metaphysical layer is made, the model speaks of what is in it as records, readings, statuses, estimates, claims and commitments, each with an origin, and asks how each came to be rather than treating any of them as simply how things are. A record can be complete and consistent and still misdescribe what it stands for.

## What a system deals in

The things a system deals in are of two kinds. Some are made by its own acts: a consult and its status, a quota grant, a command relationship, a Pod's binding to a node. They come to be when someone entitled to do so performs the act that makes them, and who may perform it says most of what matters about them. Others are what the system stands for and can know only by reading: the care a consult is meant to bring about, an airplane's angle of attack, an interconnection's frequency, which every generator and load make together and no organization sets ([grid](cases/grid-balancing.md)). Of these the system holds readings and estimates, as good as the processes that produced them. Much trouble comes from losing track of which kind a thing is, as when a status is taken for the care it records or a sensor's reading for the angle itself.

Describing an act well usually means saying what it is for, who may do it and when, what it changes, when it counts as done and what it tells whoever asked. Foundry's action types make several of these explicit, with submission criteria, rules and a transaction as the unit that commits ([Palantir](cases/palantir-ontology.md)). Schemas, types and rules are optional ways of writing a system's things down; recorded in the running system, they give designers and users a common language, as Foundry's object types and the spec and status of [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) objects do. Whatever holds the description, most often an information system, is itself physical, with its own owners, contracts and boundaries.

A definition says what it fixes and what it leaves free, and latitude belongs in it, as [mission command](cases/mission-command.md)'s intent "defines the limits within which subordinates may exercise initiative" (ADRP 6-0, 2012) and the [FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md) writes traffic management units' latitude into their authority. Latitude granted separately has an edge someone must police and tends to become a second decider. A restriction earns its place by what a realizer can derive from it, a construct belongs where the realizer must see it to choose well, and what no requirement, contract or invariant fixes is the realizer's choice, said once. A matter is usually bound when it becomes known, earlier where a commitment buys something, as quota held for months lets a [Borg](cases/borg.md) user expect production work to run.

A distinction usually earns its place where someone acts on it differently or where audit or regulation needs it, as each of [VistA](cases/vista-consults.md)'s consult statuses changes what a service or a measure does; finer detail serves people better as text. A kind that reaches an interface as a closed enumeration makes any addition a breaking change, and is better a value looked up, with "not supported in this version" a valid answer.

Not everything in the metaphysical layer needs a physical counterpart of its own. A concept of the design need not exist in the running system unless something there acts on it, and it usually earns runtime identity where it crosses a boundary: a process, a restart, concurrent owners, asynchronous completion, untrusted input, a public contract. A useful test is which behaviours can no longer be expressed without it. A system may also make part of its metaphysical layer run on purpose, as Foundry's Ontology gives people and programs shared objects and actions to query and act through, at the cost of a second path for edits, a queue, a merge rule and write-back to the source systems.

Work is best named by a canonical description that holds exactly what changes the obligation and nothing about how it is being solved, so that sharing follows from identity and demand forms a graph; a name that omits an input of the answer makes reuse silently wrong. Sharing uses what exists, then waits on what is in flight, then proposes anew, with a repair path where identity appears only during solving.

## Actors

An actor is whoever acts and answers within a system: a person, an organization, or a program that chooses among what its task leaves open, such as an AI agent. Its autonomy is set by the decision problem it actually holds, and a stable interface lets its inside change. A [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md)'s dispatchers choose which resources to move, while its governors respond by droop setting and its generation control by a fixed law; what follows a fixed law is better described by the law, and a shell interpreting commands by fixed rules is a mechanism whose users are the ones who act. Often a source's role names do the work and "actor" adds nothing, as with the [FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md)'s controllers or [VHA](cases/vista-consults.md)'s clinical services.

Under one governance, actors often organize in two ways at once: vertically by delegation, and horizontally by services and requests, allocation, shared information and arrangements such as joint doctrine's support relationships. The two need not share a shape. Joint doctrine runs operational command to the combatant commanders and administrative control through the Military Departments, so a Service component command answers to both ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)). FAA facilities answer to their service areas while the Command Center directs traffic management through units inside them. VHA's line settles who must comply and with what resources, while consults move between services as requests the line does not carry. Borg's priority bands rank kinds of work, not teams, and its quota rules apply to every team alike ([Borg](cases/borg.md)). One actor usually takes part in both: an air traffic sector works its traffic under its facility while handing aircraft to neighbours and taking restrictions from traffic management.

Many systems show only one structure or neither. Palantir's documentation describes neither, nor do SAP's sources; the [end-to-end argument](cases/end-to-end.md) and [Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md) concern layers of realization; [andon](cases/toyota-andon.md) and [SRE](cases/sre-slos.md) are mostly horizontal; [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md) has no delegation across its members. Where two structures touch, they tend to meet at a named point rather than by matching shape, as the FAA's order separates traffic management initiatives from "controller coordinated actions" at one controller's position. A source tree follows what changes together rather than either structure.

Roles come from a system's own design and from professional norms, standards, laws, doctrine, contracts and existing interfaces, and the governance decides how each acquires authority and responsibility in the system. X-Road's service client and service provider are the standard's roles, given authority by the centre's admission of a member and each provider's grant of access, neither of them delegation. The pilot in command's role is set by regulation the FAA's orders do not change. One actor can fill several roles, as US Strategic Command fills three for [GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md), and a role can pass between actors.

Authority is what an actor may decide and invoke, and capability what it can actually bring about. Designs align the two by specification and over time, as mission command pays for initiative in advance through training. Authority given to whoever holds the information works when a response stands behind it: an andon pull needs a team leader who comes, and SRE's error budget "relies on an SRE team having the authority to actually stop launches". Authority is granted at the metaphysical layer, and whether anyone uses it is settled at the physical one, by what using it has cost people before. Authority and responsibility can part, as FM 3-0 lets commanders delegate authority but not "absolve themselves of the responsibility".

A policy settles choices a mechanism or task leaves open, kept apart from the mechanism so either can change. X-Road's security policy, its trusted certification authorities and parameters such as the OCSP response lifetime, differs between instances without changing verification. Elsewhere the split is a reading: Unix's per-user shell is policy in a later gloss, SRE's "error budget policy" a whole approved document, and command in mission command a judgment not reduced to a rule. Varying only what is meant to be control shows whether a choice is open: if a result the definition should fix then changes, the repair belongs in the definition, not in a rule of the planner, and much of what a domain calls a decision is a derivation.

An extension point earns its cost when a policy will go there or a platform is built for independent evolution, as Kubernetes admits operators for custom resources. However capable the proposer, a proposal does not replace the authority that admits it: Borg's scheduler proposes placements that the master applies or refuses, and [Foundry](cases/palantir-ontology.md)'s AI agents act within scopes inherited from a user or a project. Where choices are made by improving policies, as optimizers and AI agents make them, the system owes them the conditions for good choice (what can be expressed, what switching costs, what is knowable when choosing) and the readings the policies act on, rather than a score to raise.

Decisions generally go best where the information and the ability to change the outcome meet, before the irreversible step, and are centralized where consistency, fairness, accountability or scale outweigh local knowledge; placing them by symmetry tends to get this wrong. Separation stays with the controller who holds the airspace, while the Command Center approves interfacility traffic management initiatives. Mission command moves decisions to "the point of action" and keeps precise control where synchronization is critical, and Borg schedules each cell centrally, rotating among users "to ensure fairness".

## How things come to be, and who may change them

Of anything a system keeps that matters, a balance, a status, a placement, a track, a field in an object, the question worth asking is how it comes to be and who may change it. Which acts and processes make or alter it, and who answers for them? Where there are copies, how are they kept up or derived, and how does the holder of a stale one find out? Where two places can change the same thing, what settles between them? Where it is stored is a separate and usually smaller question.

Naming one place authoritative answers a narrower question, whom to read from, and tends to stop the larger one being asked. CJCSI 3155.01C calls GCCS-J the "system of record for situational awareness", which tells everyone else where to take data from, and says nothing of which act alters a track on its way to the global picture or what settles two theatre pictures that disagree ([GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)). A system can be the place everyone reads from and still have several places where one thing is changed with nothing between them. SAP Learning's "definitive single source of truth for financial data" rests on something more particular: each posting is recorded once, and balances are derived from its line items ([SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)).

Many designs give each thing one place where it changes and have everyone else ask. In an X-Road instance an address is kept by the population register and other members request it there, since "X-Road does not change ownership of data" ([X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)). In a Borg cell, which jobs exist and which task sits on which machine changes only at the elected master; the scheduler proposes from a cached copy, and the master refuses proposals that no longer fit the state it holds ([Borg](cases/borg.md)). Joint doctrine puts the answer to who may direct a force in the act of transfer, since "forces, not command relationships, are transferred" and "two commanders may not exercise the same command relationship over the same force at any one time" ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)). An FAA handoff is completed before the aircraft enters the receiving controller's airspace and accepted by radar contact, the reply that grants approval, so that one controller at a time answers for separating the aircraft ([FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md)).

Where several actors must be able to change one thing, something has to settle between them, and the better designs make the settling an act of its own. Under Kubernetes' server-side apply the API server records which manager last set each field; another manager's change to that field is a conflict, refused unless forced, and when forced "ownership moves from its current manager to the manager making the change". A user can hand the `replicas` field to the autoscaler's controller "so they don't accidentally fight with the HorizontalPodAutoscaler (HPA) and its controller", a change of who may change the field made once so that the two never contend, and resource versions let a writer working from a stale copy find out when it writes ([Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md)). A Foundry property can change by a feed or by a user's edit, and a rule set per datasource decides which prevails ([Palantir](cases/palantir-ontology.md)). Asking who may change a thing often removes the need for an agreement protocol altogether.

What creates work is two places that can each change one thing with nothing settling between them. SAP's classic model kept a vendor balance in the line items and again in a maintained total, and financial accounting and controlling each kept documents for one transaction, so posting a vendor invoice took "fifteen consecutive steps", "ten inserts and five updates", and period end brought reconciliation. Comparing two independent readings is a different matter, a check between observations rather than a repair of copies. Neighbouring balancing authorities avoid even that at their boundary, since both act on one agreed meter and there is no second reading to reconcile ([grid](cases/grid-balancing.md)), and SRE's two teams act by agreement on the monitoring system's reading of whether an objective was met, which neither can adjust ([SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)).

A record and what it stands for are often changed by different means, and once measures read the record, the design has to tie the two together. A consult's status is changed in CPRS by people the facility has given update privileges for the service; the care it stands for is given by clinicians with a patient. Because VHA's measures read the status, the directive forbids closing consults by group update "because of the risk of closing a consult without delivering needed care", and says "Merely adding a comment without changing the status from Pending is not acceptable" ([VistA consults](cases/vista-consults.md)). One rule keeps the record from running ahead of the care, the other from lagging behind the work.

What can be derived from definitions and readable records is usually derived, and what needs keeping is mostly what crosses a boundary: durable progress, work in flight, adopted choices not yet carried out, and what only a remote system knows. A stored derived form is a performance choice with a consistency cost and a premise that can expire, a second copy that every event must update, as SAP's totals were. Kubernetes keeps no workflow: the plan is state, so a restarted controller "simply picks up where it left off", entry, takeover and a fresh start are one path, and each status records the generation of the spec it reflects.

The same question applies to a system's own parts, since who may change a program decides where a function can go. Multics kept redirection a separate command because its I/O system was built at Murray Hill and its shell at MIT, and as Ritchie recalls, "We didn't consider making changes to the shell (it was their program)." In Unix both were "under the exclusive control of Thompson", and the change took "an hour or so" ([Multics and Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)).

## Feedback loops and observation

Loops run at several tempos. A [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md)'s balance is held by inertia in seconds, governors over tens of seconds, generation control acting on area control error every few seconds and dispatchers over minutes, in timeframes that overlap, and the bias term keeps the slower loop from withdrawing the frequency support the faster one gives. Physics and fixed control laws carry the first seconds and people decide only in the later minutes, so when anyone can choose is set by what the physical layer has already done. A design aligns update rates, action times and decision frequency, and says what may happen while information is insufficient. Revision has tempos too: adaptation at each event, policies between runs, definitions deliberately and with a record.

Observation is organized around the decisions it serves: information belongs where it can change the legality, cost or future value of an available action, as [mission command](cases/mission-command.md) ties critical information requirements to decision points. Much of it reaches several layers through shared records and displays, such as area control error, an [andon](cases/toyota-andon.md) board or a common operational picture, which also help actors understand one another.

A reading is not the thing read, and it is worth what the physical process behind it is worth. What was observed, what is estimated, what is intended and what is permitted are different, and a design that keeps them apart can treat each as what it is. The 737 MAX took one vane's reading as the angle of attack, though a vane measures local airflow at one point and can be wrong, and nothing carried the reading's uncertainty to anyone who might use it ([MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md)). An estimate has a source and an uncertainty, is read by choices and is usually kept out of definitions, and a sample is not a commitment.

An action's intent, acceptance, progress and outcome are distinct observations: a [VHA](cases/vista-consults.md) consult is Pending when ordered, Active once the receiving service receives it, Partial Results while the reply is unsigned and Complete when it is signed. Completion rests on the effect or on confirmation by whatever carried it out, and in [Foundry](cases/palantir-ontology.md) the choice between a writeback webhook, which aborts the edits on failure, and a side-effect webhook, which shows no failure, decides what a success message reports. [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)'s message log shows what passed through the exchange and nothing about what the provider did.

An unknown outcome is usually kept unknown and observed again, with the uncertainty on the thing it concerns, as [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) marks a quiet node Unknown and waits five minutes by default before evicting. Silence can end a wait by policy, as that eviction or [GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)'s calls to a site silent for ten minutes do, and it settles an outcome only where a protocol gives it that meaning, as a two-phase commit coordinator aborts on a missing vote. Waiting is an answer that names what would change it, and how long to wait is a policy on an existing mechanism rather than states of its own. Estimates are kept apart from commitments: [Borg](cases/borg.md) places production work against its limits and other work against its reservation, an estimate that tracks actual use, and a machine that runs short sheds the lowest priority first. Higher-layer observations are derived by stated rules with a path back to the detail, as area control error is a formula over telemetry; where a source states none, as with GCCS-J's "fusion", saying so is where the question starts.

## Layers and delegation

Hierarchy has several sources: an interface implemented beneath an abstraction, as [Unix](cases/multics-unix.md)'s system calls offer processes and files; a function realized by assembled parts, as [GCCS-J](cases/gccs-j.md)'s global picture is fused from theatre pictures; execution spread across units, as in a [Borg](cases/borg.md) cell; control built over physical dynamics, from a [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md)'s controls to [MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md) beneath the pilot; and delegation, the vertical structure among actors. A source's "level", "echelon" or "layer" stays its own, as [Palantir](cases/palantir-ontology.md)'s "operational layer" and NERC's continuum do.

Layers have many reasons, each buying something: hiding a decision likely to change (Parnas), as making the shell an ordinary program let the command language change without the system; parallelism; load distribution; fault isolation, as small [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) controllers fail alone; scale; responsiveness; independent evolution; division of responsibility; decisions placed near local information. A higher layer often could decide as well and delegates anyway, as [mission command](cases/mission-command.md) does because information sits where the action is. A layer usually costs an interface, a failure mode and latency, and a function copied below costs more, since applications that do not need it pay and the lower layer knows less. The [end-to-end argument](cases/end-to-end.md) places a function where it can be completed and checked, which is where the requirement and the knowledge to do both coincide, "not an absolute rule, but rather a guideline". Simon's near-decomposability explains why a layer can be used through its contract alone, and why not where interactions across its boundary are strong.

Delegation turns one of an actor's actions into tasks for others, with latitude, inherited authority, constraints and an expected result, and reads what returns against what counts as done for the whole, which asks more than every subtask having ended. Mission command's intent "describes what constitutes success", so a unit can be lost without the larger task becoming undefined. A subordinate's room is made by the commander above, within the intent of the echelon above that, so no one widens their own latitude, and one who acts because orders "no longer fit the situation" owes a report back. In Kubernetes the task travels as desired state and returns as status, and a Deployment is complete when its replicas are updated and available, whatever its ReplicaSet reports. [Joint doctrine](cases/joint-command-support.md) grades the authority delegation carries: combatant command "cannot be delegated", and operational and tactical control are bounded parts of it. Results are abstracted upward, and capability and deviations are reported back. Where delegates delegate in turn, a unit acts as one actor for the command above it, with no permanent centre aggregating the whole, and the recursion stops where a part is taken as given.

The recursive form fits parts of one kind that are nearly independent. It strains where parts run jointly, where the best parts do not make the best whole, where the choice among a few large realizations matters, or where a step is a one-shot effect; mission command meets units that are not independent with control measures and Army support relationships, not more delegation. A task splits only as far as its results can be put back together, and parts exchanging intermediate results are one joint execution. Steps are not transactions: where one is refused after another has taken effect, both stand and the next derivation finds what remains, and admitting each step against the state as it then stands usually serves better than proving feasibility over the whole. Recursion in what is asked, in a method and in the runtime are different things, and most errors here report a property of the method or the runtime as one of the problem. Recursion alone is no reason for a process, owner or registry per layer. Where decomposition stops is a choice, opaque by decision differing from not yet looked at, and moving it changes the representation.

Delegation does not say how actors in different branches work together, and horizontal relations are not a gap in it. Sources use "delegate" more widely: a letter of agreement can "Delegate responsibility for control of IFR aircraft" to another facility, a horizontal transfer of airspace with no latitude or expected result. The model keeps the word for the vertical form. The FAA's delegation of certification work to Boeing's staff is that form, and what came back was documents not updated as MCAS grew.

Where layers act on one thing, each usually needs to know what the other may do. MCAS's authority over the stabilizer grew from 0.6 to 2.5 degrees while the manuals, training and safety assessment stayed behind, and what joined it to the pilot was an assumed reaction time rather than a stated rule. Asking how two parts combine is most useful where the answer turns out to be an assumption.

## Horizontal coordination

Under one governance, actors coordinate across the delegation structure by several means at once, none needing a global planner, and requests under contracts are the commonest. An [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md) member calls another's service under an access agreement, checked by the provider's security server; a [VHA](cases/vista-consults.md) service's Receive action "puts your clinic on record as accepting responsibility for completing the consult"; a controller hands an aircraft to the next sector at a point a letter of agreement fixes. The provider decides how to meet a request, which is not a delegated task even between services in one line of management, since the responsibility comes from the receiver's own act and the agreements.

Allocation divides a shared resource under a rule an allocator enforces. [Borg](cases/borg.md) keeps four things apart: priority ranks kinds of work, quota bounds what a user may ask for at a priority, admission control rejects a job its quota does not cover, and the scheduler places admitted work. Quota itself is granted and priced in capacity planning, outside Borg. Allocation within one governance can thus use price-like signals set outside the enforcing system, and a rule can be limited to stop a failure mode, as production tasks may not preempt one another, to avoid cascades. A constraint spanning actors usually needs a place of enforcement, and coupling carried as one agreed reading at each part's boundary often replaces negotiation, as a [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md) acts on its own area control error, computed from scheduled interchange and a meter agreed with each neighbour.

Shared records can carry a process's meaning rather than messages about it, as a VHA consult "acts as a depository of information about itself" and [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) loops with no delegation between them coordinate through objects they read and write.

Support relationships are set by a common superior. [Joint doctrine](cases/joint-command-support.md) has an establishing directive state purpose, resources, priority and the supported commander's degree of authority, leaves "the forces, tactics, methods, procedures, and communications" to the supporting commander, and makes the establishing authority "responsible for determining a solution" when needs cannot be met. Army doctrine's support relationships differ, so the word is used with its doctrine named.

Agreements among units are another means. [FAA](cases/faa-air-traffic.md) letters of agreement cover procedures needing more than one facility's concurrence and "only supplement" the national order; some reach an airport manager or another agency, so a contract can span the governance boundary. VHA's service agreements are written by the services and signed by their chiefs. Neither is a market.

The governance usually contributes a place for what actors cannot settle: FAA facilities take unresolved problems to higher management, [SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)'s example policy sends error budget disputes to the CTO, and a commander with joint coordinating authority can require consultation but "does not have the authority to compel agreement", so where "essential agreement cannot be obtained, the matter shall be referred to the appointing authority" ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)). A design can also give conflicting interests a settled place, as SRE's error budget lets development and operations settle release pace by one agreed number rather than a negotiation per release.

Boundaries tend to fall where ownership, execution or trust change, because each costs a protocol, a failure mode and a hand-off of state that those places need anyway, and Conway's law describes organizations' boundaries reappearing in what they build. In a system assembled from existing parts, much of its own work lies between them and goes unowned if the parts are assumed to compose by themselves. A join works when it has its own meaning and owner. X-Road gives exchange among members signed, time-stamped, logged calls, with a central server holding participation and nothing about citizens, and [Foundry](cases/palantir-ontology.md) hosts an operable model of the organization over separately kept records. Boeing's engineering and manual writers, the FAA's Flight Standardization Board and the operators depended on one another's documents, and the omission of [MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md) travelled along that dependence, which no delegation carried. Limits in a mature design gather at joins and are better met by giving the join meaning with one owner than by enlarging either side. A shared abstraction over differing mechanisms is tested against each instance it claims to cover, since who performs an act follows from who owns it.

## Where the two layers meet

A design leans on what its physical layer already does, and the first question is what that is. A [balancing authority](cases/grid-balancing.md) builds on its units' inertia and governors, physics it did not design and nobody decides in the moment, which hold the first minute of every disturbance before its own loop begins. [Kubernetes](cases/kubernetes-controllers.md) controllers can be small and restartable because everything they need is re-read from a replicated store. Operating systems, services, professions, procedures and physical processes have semantics and lifecycles a rebuild would copy worse, though coordinating them may be the system's own job, and a survey of what they expose tests a structure rather than supplying one.

The principles of whatever carries a design bound it, and much of a design is fitted to them without saying so. [Borg](cases/borg.md)'s line between compressible and non-compressible resources decides what a machine does when an estimate proves wrong: it throttles what can be shared out by slowing and, short of what cannot, terminates tasks from the lowest priority up. Production quota is limited to a cell's actual capacity, and in general bounds belong where a real resource requires them or where a restart would not converge; a bound on something that is not a resource tends to breed overflow protocols. What a mechanism caches, and for how long, sets what outage it survives: the validity of [X-Road](cases/x-road-estonia.md)'s cached global configuration bounds how long the central server may be down.

Where the carriers are people, the principles are those of management and organizations, and they bound a design as firmly. Toyota's Georgetown plant and a new Ford plant at Dearborn had similar rope and lamp, yet workers at Georgetown pulled the cord about 2,000 times a week and those at Dearborn about twice, a gap the BBC attributed to "the legacy of generations of mistrust between shop-floor workers and managers"; Ford Finance had told the plant its team-leader role might be eliminated if it did not prove its value ([andon](cases/toyota-andon.md)). The design was alike and the systems were not, because what a pull meant to the worker considering it lay in how pulls had been received. SRE's error budget likewise works only as far as people behave as its policy says ([SRE](cases/sre-slos.md)), and joint doctrine lets the level of control depend on "the associated comfort level of the commander" ([joint command](cases/joint-command-support.md)).

Somewhere a design stops describing and takes a part as given, behaving by its contract, and the outcomes the contract admits, failure, partial results and "not yet known" among them, are the design's real assumption about what lies beneath; this is a trusted boundary. MCAS took its single angle-of-attack sensor as given, with no "wrong" among its outcomes, though the fleet had seen 25 stick-shaker activations around takeoff in 17 years. It took the crew as given too, assumed to recognize a trim runaway arriving with a stick shaker and disagree alerts and to respond within seconds, a human-factors premise for which the JATR found "no studies" ([MCAS](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md)). The datagram contract names loss, reordering and duplication, and the application checks end to end what it relies on ([end-to-end](cases/end-to-end.md)). What a design takes as given can rest on people's care rather than on protection, as on early [Unix](cases/multics-unix.md), which ran without memory protection.

A property can be secured by structure or by a check, and structure usually costs less, since a check catches only what its author imagined. Unix lets each directory appear in exactly one other, so that programs walking the tree can rely on it in every state the file system reaches. Checks earn their place where separately owned or trusted parts meet, or where a wrong claim would silently corrupt something: at MIT, per-hop checksums covered the wire and not the gateways' buffers, and a gateway swapping byte pairs corrupted source files. Parts built and released together usually take each other's outputs as given, so a mismatch should fail loudly, and removing a validation does not remove its precondition. Compatibility with clients who cannot change in step is a contract paid at the boundary, as [SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)'s compatibility views keep old programs running "without changes" at a stated cost in performance.

Loss is easiest to reason about along one path, resuming from what is durable and freshly observable, as a restarted Kubernetes controller re-reads the store and fate-sharing loses a conversation's state only with its host; extra recovery paths tend to interact and hide faults. A few invariants, such as that a resource may be reused or that an effect did not happen, need positive evidence in every interleaving, which a timeout supplies only inside a protocol built for it, such as a lease with fencing tokens. The end-to-end paper keeps the observation and drops the delivery promise: acknowledgement and duplicate suppression belong to the application, which wants "I did it" or "I didn't". A repeat absorbed by name is harmless, a second real effect is not, and compensation belongs to the effect's owner. Lifecycles belong to the mechanisms that have them, nothing is reused while another actor may still reach it, and a reused mechanism is asked again about its new environment, as MCAS extended the NG's speed trim system into a new airframe.

## Reading a design

A design can be read from what is required down. Follow an important requirement to whoever answers for meeting it, and ask what that actor needs to know and may do, how its acts actually happen and how anyone sees that they have, and where a constraint spanning several actors takes effect. The [andon](cases/toyota-andon.md) reads cleanly this way: someone at the point of work able to signal, a lamp that shows the station, a team leader who answers, and a fixed-position stop where the constraint across stations takes effect. The [737 MAX](cases/boeing-737-max-mcas.md) reads with a gap: the crew held authority over the stabilizer without knowing that MCAS existed. Each step crosses both layers, since an actor is a role and also people or programs who can do the work, and an act is seen to have happened only through some physical reading.

A design is also read from its structure up, by asking what each part is for, with the reasons left open: a requirement met, a boundary spanned, a decision informed, a failure contained, a load spread, a change made independent, legibility to those who run it. The question keeps designs economical. What can be derived is derived, no two places change one thing without something settling between them, and a premise that a family of mechanisms serves is revised as one. [SAP](cases/sap-universal-journal.md)'s change of one premise about cost removed a whole family. At Boeing one premise, that crews would retrim within seconds, served the hazard classification, the simulator method, the single sensor and the manuals, and was never tested against the case the single sensor made likely. Premises like these are often physical, about what a disk can sum or how fast a person reacts, and they are the ones a design most easily forgets it holds.

The two readings correct each other: built from requirements alone, a design adds an entity for every link it follows, and trimmed from structure alone, it loses links whose invariants were out of view. Every heuristic here has an over-correction, and tensions between them are settled by what the structure is for. The aim is a more capable system with less management rather than a smaller one, since minimalism for its own sake pushes performance into special cases. A lower layer's need is better met by repairing the upper layer than by a patch below that hides the defect. Brooks's distinction sorts the rest: what is required, what crosses a boundary and what someone acts on are essential however inelegant.

A system of actors can act as one actor of a larger one and change inside while its contracts, authority and timing hold. Attention moves from what is required to what the system deals in, to who decides and observes, to what carries it out, and back, as each exposes gaps in another. The reading is itself a description, chosen for what it shows, and a design can read well at every step and still be the wrong design.
